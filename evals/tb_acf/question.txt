SYNTHETIC TEST QUESTION (evaluation suite): every district, number and file below is synthetic, generated for this test; none describes a real place. A TB programme has US$1.0 million for one round of active case finding among high-risk adults in 10 districts in 2026. For each district choose one screening algorithm: none; SYM (symptom screening, with Xpert MTB/RIF Ultra for people with presumptive TB); CXR (chest X-ray for everyone screened, with Xpert if the X-ray is abnormal). A round reaches 70% of each district's target population, and costs are per person screened. Input files: data/evals/tb_acf/districts.csv (target population), data/evals/tb_acf/acf_records_2022_2025.csv (people screened and bacteriologically confirmed cases detected, by district, year and algorithm; assume the prevalence of undiagnosed TB in the target population was stable over 2022-2025 and is unchanged in 2026), data/evals/tb_acf/algorithm_costs.csv, data/evals/tb_acf/national_reference_yields.csv. Choose the algorithms that maximise bacteriologically confirmed TB cases detected within the budget, report the total with a 95% uncertainty interval, and state the decision rule. Deliverables: allocation_optimized.csv in the run directory with columns district, package (none, SYM or CXR) and cases_detected; and the uncertainty analysis must report the total as an output named total_cases_detected.
