SYNTHETIC TEST QUESTION (evaluation suite): the population, numbers and files below are synthetic, generated for this test; they do not describe a real place. A health ministry will receive 750,000 influenza vaccine doses (10 blocks of 75,000) before next season for a population of 5 million in five age groups (0-4, 5-17, 18-49, 50-64, 65+). Allocate whole blocks to age groups, with at most 70% of any group vaccinated, to minimise expected influenza hospitalisations next season. Assume next season's transmissibility and pre-season susceptibility equal last season's, vaccination before the season with all-or-nothing protection against infection at the effectiveness given, and an age-structured SIR epidemic with the contact matrix given (mean daily contacts of a person in the row group with people in the column group). Input files: data/evals/flu_vaccine/population.csv, data/evals/flu_vaccine/contact_matrix.csv, data/evals/flu_vaccine/serosurvey_last_season.csv (pre-season susceptible titres and paired seroconversion by age group, no vaccination last season), data/evals/flu_vaccine/hospitalisations_last_season.csv, data/evals/flu_vaccine/vaccine_effectiveness.csv. Report hospitalisations averted against no vaccination, with a 95% uncertainty interval, and state the decision rule. Deliverables: allocation_optimized.csv in the run directory with columns age_group, doses (a multiple of 75,000) and hospitalisations_averted; and the uncertainty analysis must report the total as an output named hospitalisations_averted.
