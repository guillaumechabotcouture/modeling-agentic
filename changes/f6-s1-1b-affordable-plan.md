## Changelog

**Affordable plan under uncertainty (F6 S1-1b, O3).** `uncertainty.affordability` takes `enforce: true` beside `max_share_over_budget` (enforce alone is refused). With it, the analysis evaluates the uncertainty step's draws once (same Drawer, seed and number of draws) and searches budget fractions f for a plan, solved at f × the allocatable budget, that costs more than the full allocatable budget in at most the stated share of draws. It solves the unconstrained plan first; if that fails, a grid of 10 fractions from the floor of 0.5, then bisection above the highest grid fraction that meets the rule, to a tolerance of 0.005 and at most 20 ILP solves, each a recorded step. Plans at different budgets are not nested, so the result is the best visited plan that meets the rule (highest objective), not a proven optimum; the floor candidate's result is reported separately. A candidate plan's cost per draw is a sum over the precomputed cost tables. The headline plan becomes that plan. The same fraction is held back in every later solve: the re-optimised draws, stability, sensitivity, envelope decision tests (f × each tested envelope's allocatable budget, so the declared envelope reproduces the plan) and the frontier; affordability is judged against the full budget. `analysis/point.yaml` records `reserve`: f, the dollars held back, the unconstrained and affordable plans with their objectives, costs and shares over budget, the value given up (absolute and %, the percentage omitted when the unconstrained objective is not positive), and the search steps. With `enforce`, the analysis contract requires the reserve record and checks it (`analysis_contract_reserve`): plan identity, budget arithmetic, draws and seed, the planned budget of the uncertainty step, its counts and the rule. `figure_data.rebuild` restores the reserve before it solves. Claims `reserve.*` and `api.headline` carry the reserve and the value given up. When no visited fraction meets the rule the analysis is blocked (`analysis/blocked.yaml`, where `affordability`), with a reason that names what was visited and says it is not a proof that no reserve works. Without `enforce` the analysis is unchanged.
