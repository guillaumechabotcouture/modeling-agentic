## Changelog

**Change between survey rounds by group (F6 S2-3b, C3).** Unit and group targets in `calibration.joint.targets` that name one survey (`survey`, or `bias_source` with `bias_by`) are its rounds, ordered by window. `toolkit/analysis/joint_calibration.py` pairs every two rounds per group. Unit rounds are grouped by `changes.by`, else the survey's `bias_by`, else the region, and their units are weighted by `changes.weights`, else equally. Group rounds are paired per group of their table. `toolkit/calibration/history_matching.py` (`change_stats`) computes, at the best point, the observed and modelled change on the targets' scale, their signed difference and z = (modelled − observed) / sd. The modelled values are the engine's verification runs. The sd combines three parts. The first is the rounds' sampling errors, independent unless `changes.sampling_correlation` says otherwise. The second is the source offsets with signed coefficients, so an offset both rounds share cancels and offsets of different sources add. The third is the modelled change's run noise, the nugget of an emulator of the paired difference, so the covariance of one trajectory's two windows is in it. Model discrepancy is left out. The report also gives the change on the natural scale, a signed ratio and a sentence with the direction ("the model's decline is 40% smaller than observed", "the opposite sign"), plus the region's 95% range of the modelled change from the emulators. All of it goes into `survey_changes` in `analysis/joint_calibration.yaml`. A group with |z| above `survey_changes.z_max` (3, the calibration's own three-sigma cutoff, in `toolkit/calibration/thresholds.yaml`, which no evidence digest binds) is a declarable gate finding (`calibration_change_discrepancy`, through `toolkit/analysis/contract.py`). A missing record, another threshold, or a flag its numbers do not give is a finding that cannot be declared. A single round, a group missing from one round, or rounds whose metric, scale, kind, column or grouping differ are recorded as not computable with the reason. They are a declarable finding when the spec declares `calibration.joint.changes`. Specs without a survey name get one explicit not-computable entry. Tests: HM41 uses three groups with an offset of sd 0.5 shared by both rounds. The offset adds nothing to the change's sd, and a group whose survey declines while the model rises is the only one flagged, with "opposite sign". HM42 checks that run noise shared by both windows cancels in the modelled change. HM43 covers the arithmetic: a shared offset cancels, offsets of two sources add, a declared correlation applies, no declared error or no modelled value is not computable, and malformed pairs are refused. JA16 covers pairing from the spec, population weights, a missing group, different scales, a single round, refusals and the toy run's artifact. JA17 covers the gate findings.
