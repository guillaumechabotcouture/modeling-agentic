## Changelog

**Observation-target posterior: chains started at the posterior mode, one proposal per chain (hb3 triage fix 2a).** Flu R1 refused its 11-parameter observation-target posterior identically in hb1 and hb3 (adaptive Metropolis, R-hat and bulk ESS far below the floors), while R2 calibrated the same model and data. The likely cause is that the chains started from prior draws and shared one proposal covariance. New `toolkit/analysis/mode_start.py`: above `max_importance_params`, `observed_targets.fit` samples with adaptive Metropolis started at the posterior modes. The best 8 of 2^6 Sobol scan points are each moved to a mode by L-BFGS-B, with batched central-difference gradients. An optimum is verified only when its last gradient had no non-finite point. The Laplace covariance comes from a finite-difference Hessian, evaluated row by row and diagonal-only above 40 parameters, with eigenvalues floored at the prior's precision. Optima within one Laplace sd of each other are one mode. A mode is dropped only when both its peak density (20 nats) and its Laplace mass (log 1000) are far below the best one's. Every kept mode gets at least one chain, with more chains than the threshold when there are more modes, so competing modes are each sampled and R-hat compares them. Chains start overdispersed (2x) around their modes. Each chain adapts its own proposal on its own history, shrunk towards its Laplace covariance, with Cholesky jitter only when a covariance does not factor. A `posterior_mcmc` refusal is retried once from new starts before it is final, and the final message names both attempts. The convergence floors are unchanged. Up to `max_importance_params`, and when no scan point is finite, `calibration.sample` is used as before. Engine refusals during the Sobol scan are reported as `start_search_refusals`; refusals during the optimisation or the Hessian refuse the fit. `toolkit/analysis/calibration.py` is byte-identical, so the compartmental coverage pack (calibrator `calibration.posterior`, which never reaches this path) and its proof (compat_proof.py CP8) need nothing. The route a remaining refusal takes is in the calibration-refusal-route change. Self-tests MS1-MS9.
