## Changelog

**Figures and run records: one failing figure is its own, and every declared stop leaves a record.** Slice 1C-a of the v2 autonomy brief (`docs/design/v2-autonomy-brief.md`, PR 1C item 4; items 4.1-4.7 of the re-audit). `toolkit/analysis/figures.py` `render` draws, titles, saves, validates and tabulates each figure inside one try: an exception other than `Missing` becomes a `not_produced` record with `render_failed: true` and the error, the files its own attempt published are removed, never one that was already there (`_file_sigs`, `_discard`, `lib.safe_io.remove_nofollow`), every figure it opened is closed in a `finally`, and the other figures are still drawn. A declared boundary input that is refused, or that fails to load with any error, still stops the render (`PresentationRefused`, a `ValueError`; self-tests BD3, BD4, BD6, FG_BOUNDARY_ANY_ERROR). `render_findings` turns each such record into one MEDIUM, declarable finding `figure_render_failed:<figure>`; `toolkit/analysis/contract.py` reads them from the manifest-hashed `analysis/figures.yaml` (a `not_produced` that is present and not a list of records is an `analysis_contract_schema` finding). Before, any such exception ended the analysis with no manifest and left the figure open. `toolkit/calibration/figures.py` `render` does the same for the title, the in-memory render and both writes, which were outside its per-figure try, and removes only the PNG and CSV its attempt wrote. Edge values: the calibration figure's proportion axes and its emulator-error band end at 1 (`_prop_max`; above about 0.94 the band's logit was taken of values over 1 and gave NaN with a warning); a fit row whose variance is recorded as null uses its expected value (was a `TypeError`); the dollars figure draws with a zero envelope (was a `ZeroDivisionError`); cost per outcome leaves out an interval with an end that is not finite (counted apart in the caption) and a component whose cost per outcome is not finite, and keeps finite axis limits (`_axis_max`; was a `ValueError`). `toolkit/analysis/run.py` `run` records five more stops in `analysis/blocked.yaml` and a manifest with status `blocked`, with the exception's name and code, then raises them again as before: `CalibrationRefused`, `CalibrationInputError`, `AnchorError`, `OptimizerError` and `BatchRefused` (`_stop_where`); any other error is not recorded, and a record or log call that fails never replaces the stop raised. Not in this slice: `_infra_batch` reading `analysis/boundary/` summaries (item 4.8 needs an edit to `agents/stage_machine.py`), the boundary retry (4.9), the solver gap, the model clip guards and history matching. Self-tests FG_ISOLATE (a-d), FG_HIGH_PREV, FG_VARIANCE_NONE, FG_PROP_AXIS, FG_LOW_PROP_BAND, FG_BOUNDARY_ANY_ERROR, FG_ZERO_ENVELOPE, FG_AXIS_FINITE, FG_WITHHELD_MIXED, FG_COMPONENT_FINITE, FG_DISCARD_OWN (`figures.py`), CF6, CF7 (`calibration/figures.py`), K9b, K9c (`contract.py`), RUN_BLOCKED_CAL (five cases), RUN_BLOCKED_OTHER, RUN_BLOCKED_UNRECORDED, RUN_BLOCKED_RERUN (`run.py`). γ to θ are the tests and fixes from three Codex review rounds; the third round's fixes were not reviewed again.
