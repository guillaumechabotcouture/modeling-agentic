## Changelog

**A standing check of the units' coordinates and areas against boundaries (design note `docs/design/v2-input-geometry.md`).** In a real run, the geography input table had been built from a boundary set in which 4 pairs of unit names were attached to each other's polygons, so each unit in a pair carried its partner's coordinates, area and population, and nothing in the harness caught it. `toolkit/spec/geometry.py` checks the spec's units table against the boundaries declared in the run's unsigned presentation file (the figures' `boundaries: {path, feature_id, crosswalk, credit}` block; columns from `decision_units.coordinates: {lat, lon, area_km2}` or named lat/lon/area_km2), in pure Python and json: ray casting with holes and MultiPolygon parts, distances on a plane scaled by cos(latitude), and areas by equal-area integration (exact for edges straight in longitude/latitude, unchanged by resampling). Codes: `reciprocal_swap` (HIGH), `point_outside_own_polygon` (HIGH beyond `edge_tolerance_km`, LOW edge case within it), `area_mismatch` (MEDIUM beyond `area_ratio_max`, HIGH when the area matches the polygon the point falls in), `unmatched` (MEDIUM), `geometry_check_failed` (HIGH, fail closed on malformed or degenerate inputs) and `geometry_check_not_run` (LOW: never a silent pass). Thresholds (5 km, 1.5) are in `effort_floors.yaml` `input_checks.geometry`, not in `toolkit/analysis/thresholds.yaml`, which is hashed into every benchmark declaration digest. The harness writes `data/input_checks.json`; `h_SPEC` refuses a spec with a HIGH it neither fixes nor proposes as an exception, the gate re-runs the check through `harness_findings`, and the sign-off sheet lists the counts, the proposals, the approvals and the open findings. `decision_units.geometry_accepted` holds proposals: a MEDIUM one is accepted, a HIGH one counts only once the owner approves it (the owner's sign-off, or `geometry.py approve <run_dir> --by owner`), recorded in `data/geometry_signoff.json` and bound to the evidence hash of the inputs. A lead sign-off approves nothing and the run pauses at `awaiting_signoff`. Every sign-off (spec only, spike, or the brief with a spec) freezes the geometry declaration and the boundary, crosswalk and units sha256s; a later change is `geometry_inputs_changed_after_signoff` (HIGH), approvals lapse with their evidence, and a signed spec without a geometry record is `geometry_record_missing` (HIGH; the owner's `approve` creates it). Enforcement (defence in depth, not authentication; owner-signed approvals in the next PR are the boundary): the owner's commands and records are denied to every agent at the v1 tool hook in every sign-off mode; the owner CLIs take no default signer and no abbreviated flag; no Bash call is credited with the geometry records or the brief's sign-off outputs; indirect execution is a self-test XFAIL (SO13b). Lead mode: no agent runs `decision_brief.py sign`. The lead writes `decision_brief_lead_answers.yaml` (only the lead may: `enforcement.OWNED_BY`), the PostToolUse hook records the lead's own write with its sha256 in `trace.jsonl`, and the harness (`main.py harness_sign_brief_lead`) signs only a strictly typed file the lead wrote in the current session, registers what signing wrote with the integrity record, and otherwise says exactly what is missing; the procedure is in the lead's prompt and in the sign denial. One loader: `toolkit/spec/geometry.py` is also the figures' boundaries parser and GeoJSON reader (#127's keys and refusals: a feature mapped to two units, a duplicate feature id, a non-geographic CRS, out-of-range coordinates; an empty MultiPolygon leaves its unit unmatched), maps draw holes, and `crosswalk.union` is removed: a unit mapped to more than one feature is refused, naming it (a union's area needs exact polygon intersection, and nothing uses it yet). Tests were written first: IG1-IG14 (`toolkit/spec/geometry.py`), BD1-BD7 (`toolkit/analysis/figures.py`), IG-M1-M5 (`agents/stage_machine.py`), IG-S1-S2 (`toolkit/spec/signoff.py`), DB35-DB36 (`scripts/decision_brief.py`) and SO11-SO17 (`main.py`, through the real hooks). A local check against the real run's files found the 4 pairs (counts only in the PR).
