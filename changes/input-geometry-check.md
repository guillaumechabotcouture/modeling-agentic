## Changelog

**A standing check of the units' coordinates and areas against boundaries (design note `docs/design/v2-input-geometry.md`).** In a real run, the geography input table had been built from a boundary set in which 4 pairs of unit names were attached to each other's polygons, so each unit in a pair carried its partner's coordinates, area and population, and nothing in the harness caught it. `toolkit/spec/geometry.py` checks the spec's units table against the boundaries declared in the run's unsigned presentation file (the figures' `boundaries: {path, feature_id, crosswalk, credit}` block; columns from `decision_units.coordinates: {lat, lon, area_km2}` or named lat/lon/area_km2). It uses pure Python and json: ray casting with holes and MultiPolygon parts, distances on a plane scaled by cos(latitude), and areas by equal-area integration. Codes: `reciprocal_swap` (HIGH), `point_outside_own_polygon` (HIGH beyond `edge_tolerance_km`, LOW edge case within it), `area_mismatch` (MEDIUM beyond `area_ratio_max`, HIGH when the area matches the polygon the point falls in), `unmatched` (MEDIUM unit without a polygon, HIGH polygon claimed twice), `geometry_check_failed` (HIGH, fail closed on malformed inputs) and `geometry_check_not_run` (LOW: never a silent pass). Thresholds (5 km, 1.5) are in `effort_floors.yaml` `input_checks.geometry`, not in `toolkit/analysis/thresholds.yaml`, which is hashed into every benchmark declaration digest. The harness writes `data/input_checks.json` (harness-owned). `h_SPEC` refuses a spec with a HIGH that it neither fixes nor proposes as an exception, and the gate re-runs the check through `harness_findings`. The sign-off sheet lists the counts, each acceptance and the open findings. `decision_units.geometry_accepted: {<finding id>: reason}` in the spec holds proposals. A MEDIUM proposal is accepted. A HIGH one counts only once the owner approves it (after the Codex review of #131): the owner's sign-off, or `geometry.py approve <run_dir> --by owner` after it, records the approval in the harness-owned `data/geometry_signoff.json`, bound to the evidence hash of the inputs. A lead sign-off approves nothing, and the run pauses at `awaiting_signoff`. Every sign-off freezes the geometry declaration (enabled or not, boundaries and crosswalk as declared, columns) and the boundary, crosswalk and units sha256s. A later change is `geometry_inputs_changed_after_signoff` (HIGH), and approvals lapse with their evidence; labels stay unsigned. A stale or reasonless proposal is `geometry_acceptance_invalid`. The review also brought these fixes: degenerate rings fail closed; a reciprocal pair forms only when both points lie outside their own polygons (nested polygons crashed the pair test); and areas use equal-area integration, exact for edges straight in longitude/latitude and unchanged by resampling (the mean-latitude scaling moved with vertex density). Codex round 2: the owner's commands (`geometry.py approve`, `stage_machine.py sign`, `signoff.py sign`, `scope_signoff.py sign`, `decision_brief.py sign --by owner`, by path, module or Python call) are denied to every agent at the v1 tool hook in every sign-off mode. The geometry records and `decision_spec.signoff.json` are v1 script outputs, so they are protected and watched by the integrity record. `decision_brief.py sign` freezes the spec's geometry when there is a spec. A signed spec without a geometry record is HIGH `geometry_record_missing`, and the owner's `approve` creates the record. Owner authentication itself is held for the owner's decision. Tests were written first: SO11a-c (`main.py`, through the real PreToolUse hook), DB35 (`scripts/decision_brief.py`), IG13, IG1-IG12 (`toolkit/spec/geometry.py`), IG-M1-M5 (`agents/stage_machine.py`) and IG-S1-S2 (`toolkit/spec/signoff.py`). A local check against the real run's files found the 4 pairs (counts only in the PR).
