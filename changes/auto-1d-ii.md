## Changelog

**Compute durability (v2 autonomy brief, PR 1D item 2; `scripts/compute.py` only).** A scheduler call whose connection drops (`submit`, the demand markers, `wait`) is tried again up to `MA_COMPUTE_COMM_RETRIES` (5) times over `MA_COMPUTE_COMM_WAIT_S` (120 s) while the dask client reconnects, and a retried chunk keeps its task key so nothing is placed twice; before, the first drop ended the batch and charged what was in flight. A scheduler view that stays unread with nothing out now stops the call after `MA_COMPUTE_NO_WORKER_WAIT_S` with the queued inputs failed as infra, instead of spinning. A chunk whose future fails with `KilledWorker` or `CancelledError` before the next view shows its worker retired on purpose is treated as a scale-down: no infra retry used, the same processes. On a tagged pool the bundle is scattered to compute workers only, and waits for the first one when none is up. The bundle leaves out logs and the records `compute.py` writes beside a batch (summaries, partial rows, job records, the ledger); every file left out is one the code fingerprint ignores (`_bundle_skips` builds on `_fp_ignored`, now the fingerprint's single skip rule). A dead caller on another host whose record carries a heartbeat is charged, per remote chunk, the time from the chunk's start to that heartbeat, at most its share (an estimate; `inflight_basis` in the ledger record). A call records `cap_outlook`, its projected core-seconds against its allowance, from the ledger's history before launch and from its own finished tasks while it runs, and warns on stderr when the projection passes the allowance; no cap is added or removed. Self-tests CD1-CD9 run against a fake dask client. Merging changes the compute code fingerprint once (`_map_dask`, `make_bundle` and `map_fn` are hashed), so stored engine rows made before the merge are not reused after it.
