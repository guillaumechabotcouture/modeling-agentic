## Changelog

**Case management as an implementable package (F6 S2-2, C2, C7; malaria engine v0.12).** In F5 the case-management add-on `cm_add` was a fixed, unsourced +0.15 to effective treatment for all ages from day one, and the same rise lifted severe-care access one for one. With the new fixed switch `cm_target = 1`, the add-on applies only in ages [`cm_age_lo_m`, `cm_age_hi_m`) months (default 2-59, the iCCM age band; Oresanya et al. 2019, Isiguzo et al. 2019). It ramps in linearly over `cm_ramp_years` (default 2.5, card range 2-3; an assumption from the RAcE programme timing) from the delivery start. It raises severe-care access only by `cm_severe_link` x the realised gain. `cm_severe_link` defaults to 0, because no source we found estimates it (Oo et al. 2019 pools no severe-malaria outcome); with 0, the add-on changes deaths only through transmission and post-treatment prophylaxis. `cm_add` stays a scenario parameter with point value 0.15, gains the card range [0, 0.15] and sources: unit-wide low 0.03, central 0.07 and high 0.15, derived from catchment gains of +0.15 to +0.40 and an assumed catchment share of 0.2-0.5. Two cost drivers are added for the spec's prices (S1-3): `cm_child_years`, children 2-59 months covered while the package's add-on runs, charged even at `cm_add` 0; and `treated_cm_ages`, treated episodes in those ages, for commodity costs. The card lists both under a new `outputs.cost_drivers_when: {driver: {parameter: value}}` (they exist only with `cm_target` 1, and the contract test requires every `cost_drivers` entry in every run); the spec validator refuses a mapping to one unless the run sets the parameter (`toolkit/spec/schema.py` `conditional_driver_problems`, SP-CD1), and `agents/analysis_backend.py` reads both lists. Every option acts after the fork and fails closed outside its domain (`model.cm_check`). `priors.yaml` gives each new fixed parameter a reason. ENGINE_NOTES.md (v0.12) gives, for each part, the standard model it follows or why it departs (Griffin 2010/2014; the national Nigeria EMOD model, Ozodiegwu et al. 2023) and the cost sources (Collins et al. 2014, Daviaud et al. 2017). With `cm_target` 0, the default, a run is byte-identical to v0.11: `benchmarks/restamp_proof.py` found v0.11 (bb5cd80) and v0.12 (bcaa7cc) outputs identical in 23 of 23 cases (14 pack settings, 5 evidence cases, 4 pinned scenarios with case-management arms; local run `local-v012-2`), and the four evidence files were re-stamped (`validate_once.sh` confirmation pending). Tests, written first and failing before the implementation: engine C0-C7 (`selftest_late.py`).

## Status

- **S2-2:** the malaria engine's case-management add-on targets declared ages with a ramp, lifts severe-care access only through a declared link (default 0), and reports the cost drivers the CM prices need. The F6 spec sets `cm_target: 1`, `cm_add` with its range, and a range for `cm_severe_link`.
