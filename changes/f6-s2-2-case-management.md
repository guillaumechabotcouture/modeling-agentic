## Changelog

**Case management as an implementable package (F6 S2-2, C2, C7; malaria engine v0.12).** In F5 the case-management add-on `cm_add` was a fixed, unsourced +0.15 to effective treatment for all ages from day one, and the same rise lifted severe-care access one for one. With the new fixed switch `cm_target = 1`, `cm_add` is the gain inside the iCCM catchment (card range [0, 0.40]; about +0.15 to +0.40 in Oresanya et al. 2019, Isiguzo et al. 2019 and Oo et al. 2019). Inside the catchment treatment is capped at 0.95 (severe-care access at 1) and the unit value is the share-weighted mean with `cm_catchment_share` (new, default 0.3, card range 0.2-0.5, an assumption): (1 - s) base + s min(cap, base + gain). It applies only in ages [`cm_age_lo_m`, `cm_age_hi_m`) months (default 2-59, the iCCM age band) and ramps in linearly over `cm_ramp_years` (default 2.5, range 2-3, an assumption from the RAcE programme timing) from the delivery start. A delivery period that stops earlier ends the gain at the stop. Severe-care access rises only by `cm_severe_link` x the realised gain, saturating at 1. `cm_severe_link` defaults to 0, because no source we found estimates it (Oo 2019 pools no severe-malaria outcome); with 0, the add-on changes deaths only through transmission and post-treatment prophylaxis. Two conditional cost drivers are added, listed under a new card field `outputs.cost_drivers_when: {driver: {parameter: value}}` because they exist only with `cm_target` 1: `cm_child_years`, child-years inside the catchment (the same share as the effect) while the package's add-on runs, charged even at `cm_add` 0; and `treated_cm_ages`, a diagnostic. The cost recipe (`engine.CM_COST_ITEMS`, on the card) charges Collins et al. 2014's recurrent total per child-year, which already includes medicines and supplies, on `cm_child_years`, with no separate ACT/RDT item; its `includes` make `costs.py` refuse one as a double count. Start-up per CHW is a `per_unit` item from the spec's CHW count. A predicate may name only a `fixed` engine parameter, and the spec validator refuses a mapping to a conditional driver unless it holds at the point value, over every range or drawn prior and in every `parameter_value` decision test (`schema.parameter_domain`, `conditional_driver_problems`, SP-CD1-2). `toolkit/engines/conditional_drivers.py` is the contract test for the new field (structure; each driver present and finite under its predicate; CD1-CD4). It sits beside `contract.py` because `contract.py` is part of every engine's code fingerprint. `agents/analysis_backend.py` reads both lists. Every option acts after the fork and fails closed outside its domain (`model.cm_check`); `priors.yaml` gives each new fixed parameter a reason. ENGINE_NOTES.md (v0.12) gives, for each part, the standard model it follows or why it departs (Griffin 2010/2014; the national Nigeria EMOD model, Ozodiegwu et al. 2023) and its sources. With `cm_target` 0, the default, a run is byte-identical to v0.11: `benchmarks/restamp_proof.py` found v0.11 (bb5cd80) and v0.12 (54db16e) outputs identical in 23 of 23 cases (14 pack settings, 5 evidence cases, 4 pinned scenarios with case-management arms; local run `local-v012-4`), and the four evidence files were re-stamped (`validate_once.sh` confirmation pending). Tests, written first and failing before each fix: engine C0-C11 (`selftest_late.py`), including hand-counted boundary cases (ages 2 and 60 months, a ramp cut by the delivery stop, severe-care saturation, a longer outcome window, windows and drivers at `cm_add` 0); schema SP-CD1-2; conditional_drivers CD1-CD4. Codex review round 1: no P1, four P2s and a P3; round 2: no P1, two P2s (cap order under partial coverage; predicates on fitted parameters). Each was fixed test-first (C8f-g, CD5, SP-CD3).

## Status

- **S2-2:** the malaria engine's case-management add-on targets declared ages with a ramp, lifts severe-care access only through a declared link (default 0), and charges the same catchment share it credits, with a cost recipe that cannot double-count commodities. Owner decision O2: CM is an allocatable package with a sourced range and linked costs. The F6 spec sets `cm_target: 1`, `cm_add` and `cm_catchment_share` with their ranges, and a range for `cm_severe_link`.
