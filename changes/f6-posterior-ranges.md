## Changelog

**A declared net-effect posterior's own hull sets its columns' draw ranges and design box; the posterior is a sign-off precondition.** Owner decision, 14 Nov. The F6 sample (sha256 d45eebb2…64aa) was refused because its columns fell outside the draw ranges the Drawer passed: cfp_kill and irs_kill were checked against the ranges in `fitted.yaml`, and cfp_half against the spec's range. `batch.design_box` used the same narrow ranges. Now one function, `net_effect_fit.posterior_ranges`, computes each column's range from the loaded, verified sample: its hull, optionally widened by `uncertainty.net_effect_posterior.margin` (a share of the hull's width) and never past the column's hard bounds. `uncertainty.net_effect_sample` loads the sample once per spec and applies those ranges, through `with_posterior_ranges`, wherever execution reads a range: the Drawer, `core.Analysis` (draws, sensitivity ends, automatic range tests), `batch.design_box` (with the emulator domain, through `design_ranges`) and the evidence dossier's execution context. `load_posterior` keeps its sha256, finalize() replay, seeds and engine-identity checks. Its check against draw ranges is replaced by `hard_bounds`: the bounds the card's units imply (a probability or share in [0, 1], a duration of at least 0) and the priors.yaml range, as before. The manifest's engine record (`net_effect_posterior_ranges`), the design (`posterior_ranges`), the uncertainty artifact and `uncertainty_report.yaml` record that these ranges came from the posterior, with its sha256 (`range_source: posterior_hull`). A new precondition, `net_effect_posterior`, is registered in `toolkit/spec/preconditions.py`. It loads the posterior through the same path and checks that the run's design box covers it. Signing, the signed status that launch and resume read, and the analysis's validation all run it, so the owner can no longer sign a spec whose posterior the run refuses only at analysis start. Specs without the field are unchanged; the toy design hash recorded before the change is reproduced. These tests were written first and failed before the change: F15 (two hard-bound refusals replace the draw-range case) and F28 in net_effect_fit, NP5–NP7 in uncertainty, BA-NP2–3 in batch, and PC12–PC15 in preconditions. RNP1–2 in run.py (an end-to-end toy run) were written alongside the change. The real staged sample was replayed locally through the Drawer's path, with no engine run, and loads: 65 rows (α). Codex's read-only review raised two P1s and three P2s, all fixed with their cases written first and failing (β). P1s: a decision test's values no longer widen a posterior column's box, and the precondition refuses a parameter_value test that sets a column outside its range (BA-NP4, PC16); the precondition also refuses a run whose point value of a posterior column lies outside its range (PC17). P2s: a replaced prior is drawn as uniform over the hull, because a log-uniform over a hull reaching zero is undefined (NP8); the sample cache is keyed on engine identity (NP9); a margin never cuts inside the hull (F28f).
