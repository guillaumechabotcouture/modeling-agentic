## Changelog

**The lead's provisional spec amendment (#330) after Codex round 3 of #342 (findings 3-12).** `toolkit/spec/amend.py` checks the patched spec's cited paths (inside the run) and its named engine's signed configuration (within its limits) before `schema.validate` reads a cited file or builds the engine (`_guards`). Recovering an interrupted hand-off checks the parent's signature again and resolves the child beside the parent only, never at the recorded absolute path, so a restored copy of the parent alone is refused and the original tree is not written. The child is bound to the parent's final `run_state.json` bytes before they are saved (`_go(before_save=...)`, `_state_bytes`), so no interruption leaves a FINAL parent whose child binds its paused state. Strict child lookup requires the matching parent digest; the storage fallback (`handoff_chain(kept=True)`) follows only a recorded refusal and a child made from this parent's spec. `run_v2` holds the parent's amendment lock through the hand-off and the child's run, `Machine()` reads and writes back a lead run's state under the lock, and `main.py main_v2` takes the lock before it stamps metadata: a resume another process holds exits 1 with nothing stamped, recorded as a crash or uploaded (`run_v2_and_store` neither records nor stores an `AmendmentLocked`). A child's provisional heading names its parent beside it when the two were moved together. Codex round 1 of #349: recovery and storage accept only the child the rethink request made (its signed spec digest and amendment id, `_made_by`) and never a linked sibling; a hand-off interrupted before the parent is FINAL stores its signed child; a failed lineage write is raised and leaves the parent at SPEC, recoverable; a child another process holds stops storage at that child while the finished parent is still stored (`main._store_chain(upto=)`, `AmendmentLocked.run_dir`); a moved manual amendment names its parent by the parent-state digest. Codex round 2 of #349: the child is matched by its patch content too (`amend` records `patch_sha256` in the child metadata) and by the parent's own sign-off record (`_own_spec_digest`); a failed lineage write also restores the machine's state and the parent metadata, so a retry on the same machine finishes the hand-off; the chain is stored one run at a time (`iter_handoff_chain`), stopping at an unreadable run or at a child whose lock another process holds; a store callback without `upto` stores the parent alone; an unfinished hand-off stores only a lead-signed child with its state; a damaged parent state keeps a recorded refusal's child.
