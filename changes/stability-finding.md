## Changelog

**The toolkit path reads its own allocation-stability record (check-value item 5).** `toolkit/analysis/run.py` writes `analysis/stability.yaml` (leave-one-group-out allocation stability), but no harness finding read it: `allocation_unstable` existed only in the spike backend (`agents/analysis_backend.py`), which no v2 run uses. New `toolkit/analysis/contract.py stability_findings(run_dir)`, added by `toolkit/analysis/api.py gate_findings` after the decision-test findings (not a contract finding: `gate_findings` is unchanged). It reads the record once, with no link followed, hashed on the bytes parsed against a completed manifest. (1) An UNSTABLE package-identity or value verdict is a HIGH `allocation_unstable` (key `stability`), scope-declarable and decision-bearing. Its claim names the groups whose exclusion changes the plan, the worst fold's units and share, and the share of units that change package in at least one fold. (2) NOT_RUN on an allocation deliverable (not `deliverable_kind: estimates`) is a MEDIUM `stability_not_run` that states the reason (the skip reason or each fold's), marked `fix_task: False`. (3) A record that cannot be verified or read, an unknown verdict, verdicts that disagree on NOT_RUN, or a verdict without fold records is a non-declarable HIGH `analysis_contract_stability` (fail closed). Routing: `agents/stage_machine.py OWNER_DECLARE_KINDS` (the toolkit's decision-bearing `allocation_unstable` only; the spike's keeps the policy map). `apply_owner_rule` marks it `owner_resolution` with `owner_disposition: declare`, so rule 2o asks the owner at the first gate, as #357 does for `decision_test_changes_plan`. The owner item binds the claim, the record's path and hash (`contract.stability_record`) and the disposition `declare_with_dependence_stated`. It is not also put through `scripts/scope_signoff.py`'s policy map, which would ask it again under another fingerprint. An accept does not let the run ACCEPT. `scripts/validate_critique_yaml.py _recompute_action` moves the item to `owner_declared_high` (not `owner_accepted_high`), and a gate that would ACCEPT declares it (new rule 4d). `apply_medium_rule` reads 4d as ACCEPT, so open findings with a test still go to the fix loop (4m) and the rest are declared (3m). `open_findings` lists the item untested, and a `fix_task: False` finding too, so both reach `declared_findings.yaml`. `declared_dependence_lines` heads the report, after the plan-changing tests, with each accepted item: the claim and who accepted it (the lead's provisional accepts say so). `agents/findings_ledger.py owner_resolved` gives the item the plan-changing test's ledger lifecycle: it is closed when a gate no longer reports it and reopened when a gate reads it as asked again. A decline is HIGH `scope_declaration_declined`, as before. Retro (read-only, the 31 v2 runs of the check-value analysis: hb0 to hb2, the earlier evals, Nigeria F2 to F6 run 4). 8 would raise `allocation_unstable`: 7 Nigeria runs and 1 hb run. 14 would raise `stability_not_run`: 8 hb runs and 6 earlier runs. 9 raise nothing: 7 estimates runs with no stability record, 1 ROBUST run, and 1 Nigeria run whose UNSTABLE record sits under a manifest with no completion status, which the contract checks already report. None raise `analysis_contract_stability` through `api.gate_findings` (the old-manifest run's is dropped beside the contract's own). Codex round 1 (8 P1s, all fixed): the claim says when the folds are counted against the point-value plan (`deliverable.plan: expected_value`); the sign-off page and the provisional list treat the item as measured and plan-changing, not untested (`disposition: declare`); the owner item binds the record the claim was read from (`stability_record` on the finding), never a second read; the report states an accept only while the record there now has the accepted fingerprint, else says it is asked again; an owner-declared finding a later gate fixed leaves the report's declared list; a spec without a completed manifest is a finding (NO_MANIFEST, dropped beside the contract's), not 'nothing to check'; the artifact is found by kind, under any name; fold records are validated (group, unit count, changed units and share consistent with one unit total). P2: the NOT_RUN remedy fits its reason. Left for a follow-up (P2): the fix planner can still plan a task for a `fix_task: False` finding when another finding sends the gate to the fix loop. Self-tests K-ST10, K-ST11 and an extended SO-ST5 cover the round. Self-tests K-ST1 to K-ST9 (`toolkit/analysis/contract.py`), AP11 (`toolkit/analysis/api.py`), OR-V8 (`scripts/validate_critique_yaml.py`), SO-ST1 to SO-ST6 and the updated T36 (`agents/stage_machine.py`).
