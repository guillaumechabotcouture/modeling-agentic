## Changelog

**Benchmark bands come from their sources, and stored evidence is bound to them (F6 S1-2, critique C4).** Every trial or cost benchmark in the malaria pack declares the estimand its source reports (`estimand_type`: odds, risk, rate or hazard ratio, or cost per outcome). Each also records either its published interval as given (`published: {lower, upper, level, scale: ratio | currency_per_outcome, table, unit}`, with the source's diagnostic and age band, 'mixed' or 'not reported' where so) or a `band_override` with its reason, and the band is derived from that record. `toolkit/engines/benchmark_sources.py` (self-test BS1–BS53) fails on a band that differs from its derivation; on an entry with neither source nor override; on a malformed entry or a missing or unknown estimand type; on a source estimand that the engine's estimand does not match, without an `estimand_mapping` note, with or without an override; and on a `requires` interval that differs from the published one. The exemptions are listed: oracle and rule types, and unsourced entries in packs whose engine is a self-test toy. The check runs in `benchmark.validate()` and `Runner()` (so in every direct caller) and in the harness step `spec_benchmarks`. Joint calibration computes a benchmark target's sd at the band's recorded interval level. Results rows carry `band_sha256` and `fitted.yaml` carries `band_declarations`: digests of each benchmark's declaration as resolved in the pack (band and source fields, the engine estimand and observable, its arms, settings, configurations, horizon, seeds and engine), and for a fit also the fit rule. Stale evidence ("rerun required" for a row, "refit required" for fitted values, including a changed fit-target set or fit rule, fitted values with no readable pack, or a configured fitted file that is missing for an engine with fitted parameters) is refused by `verified_benchmarks`, `verified_spec_benchmarks` and its producer, estimates acceptance, `merge`, `rescore`, the `run` CLI, `render_notes`, `b15_mechanisms`, the `engine_parameters benchmarks` step, the analysis entry (blocked: "refit required: <ids>") and `fitted_ranges`, which every analysis read of fitted values goes through. Seven bands were symmetric around the estimate and wider than the published 95% interval, and are corrected: B7 0.71–0.98 (was 0.68), B8 0.48–0.64 (0.46), B9 0.21–0.65 (0.09), B10 0.30–0.67 (0.23), B11 0.42–0.70 (0.38), B12 0.42–0.88 (0.34), B12b 0.61–1.23 (0.49). B20 now uses Mosha 2022's public-provider 95% uncertainty interval of $1–105 per DALY averted (was 3.8–95). B13 keeps 0.14–0.40 under an override pending owner review, because the pooled fixed-effect CI of 0.25–0.29 understates the heterogeneity (I² 94%). B17–B19 bands are the target divided and multiplied by 5; their sources' own intervals have not been checked, so they are marked pending_source_check. The engine code is unchanged. The stored malaria artifacts now report "refit required: B10, B11, B12, B12b, B12m, fit_rule" (`fitted.yaml`) and stale scores for all 16 trial and cost rows (`results.yaml`), so a malaria analysis is blocked until F6 refits and re-runs the pack (not done here). The compartmental and renewal packs are unaffected.
