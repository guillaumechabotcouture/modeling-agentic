## Changelog

**Benchmark bands come from their sources (F6 S1-2, critique C4).** Each trial benchmark in the malaria pack records its published interval as given (`published: {lower, upper, level, scale, table}`), its `estimand_type` (odds, risk, rate or hazard ratio) and, where the source gives them, its `diagnostic` and `age_band`. The band is derived from that interval unless the entry records a `band_override` with its reason. `toolkit/engines/benchmark_sources.py` (self-test BS1–BS22) fails on any band that differs from its derivation. It also fails on a ratio, odds-ratio, interaction or cost benchmark with neither source nor override, on a published estimand type that the engine's estimand does not match without an `estimand_mapping` note, and on a `requires` interval that differs from the published one. The check runs in the benchmark runner (`validate`, `fit`, `run`, `merge`, `rescore`), in the harness step `spec_benchmarks`, and in joint calibration's benchmark targets. Seven bands were symmetric around the estimate and wider than the published 95% interval: B7 0.71–0.98 (was 0.68), B8 0.48–0.64 (0.46), B9 0.21–0.65 (0.09), B10 0.30–0.67 (0.23), B11 0.42–0.70 (0.38), B12 0.42–0.88 (0.34) and B12b 0.61–1.23 (0.49). The plan named four of them; the other three were found while confirming the sources. B13 keeps 0.14–0.40 under an override pending owner review, because the pooled fixed-effect CI of 0.25–0.29 understates the heterogeneity (I² 94%). B17–B20 record that no interval is published. B10, B11, B12 and B12b are fit targets, so `benchmarks/fitted.yaml` (`cfp_kill`, `irs_kill`) and `results.yaml` must be refitted and re-run before F6 (not done here). At the stored values, B12b (0.579) is now outside its band.
