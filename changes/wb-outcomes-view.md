## Changelog

**Review workbench: Outcomes view, and the comparison between runs.** `views/outcomes.py` with one adapter per deliverable kind (`outcomes_package.py`, `outcomes_estimates.py`), its checks and the comparison (`outcomes_checks.py`, `outcomes_compare.py`). It shows the headline as point, median and interval and as a share of the comparator; the plan's package mix with each option's cost rank from the signed spec; every decision test with what was varied, its verdict against the run's own thresholds and the register row it is linked to; stability, value of information by input, plan variants, burden against external references and the emulator's boundary check; for an estimate, the outputs and the threshold decision. Twelve checks on one generic model, all for the reader to judge unless a record contradicts itself: the dearest option chosen nearly everywhere (the producer's own share and minimum), a headline above half the comparator, a point value far from the median or outside the interval, plan-changing and near-miss tests and whether an issue carries them, burden references outside their band, value of information that is indicative or rests on an input the data do not constrain, components the boundary check did not cover, stability verdicts that disagree, tests under which the point plan cannot be applied, a variant over budget, fixed inputs that change the plan. **Comparison**: `&against=<run>` compares two runs of one question (package units, headline, envelope, comparator) and diffs the two verified signed specs key by key; a shift with no key that differs is a problem, and runs of different questions are never compared. The page gains a generic control for a parameter the reader chooses. `--accept` passes on four new acceptance files (two allocation runs on different engines, an estimate, and one run against its predecessor) and the earlier thirteen are unchanged. Stacked on the fixtures.
