## Changelog

**A `burden_scale` decision test (F6 run 3, owner decision D-c).** F6 run 2's critics found that the baseline burden that ranks the units is the engine's own (D-002). Its comparator-arm under-5 malaria deaths are 1.2 to 1.8 times the external zone estimate in the two checked zones, and 4 to 7 times it in the three report-only zones. The burden anchor (S2-5d) needs all-ages rows, and F6's references are under-5 only. So the owner declared a departure from principle 3 (baseline burden from external estimates) and asked for a decision test that scales zone burden. No existing type does this: `calibration_target` moves prevalence targets, `outcome_ratio` pairs packages, and `unit_attribute` feeds engine attributes. The new type, `burden_scale: {references: [validation.burden row ids], shares: [s in (0, 1]]}` (`toolkit/analysis/decision_tests.py` `_burden_scale`), takes for each listed row its factor, the row's value over the model's point value for it (the comparison `burden_validation` reports). Every package's outcomes in the units of the row's group are then multiplied by factor^s. The engine's relative reductions are kept, benefits scale with the burden, costs and every other unit are held, and the plan is re-solved. It reads the point outputs only, so no engine job and no new evaluation. The record gives each row's group, unit count, model and external values and factor, plus a check unit per group. When a row's outcome or age band is not the objective's, the record states as an assumption that its ratio is applied to the objective's outcome. `burden_scale_refs`, shared by sign-off (`toolkit/spec/schema.py`) and the run, refuses: a test without validation.burden; an unknown row; a row that is not an estimated checked or report-only row; a unit in the groups of two listed rows; and shares outside (0, 1]. Self-tests SP-BS1-7 (schema) and BS-R1-3 (`run.py`, burden e2e toy) failed first, because the type was unknown.
