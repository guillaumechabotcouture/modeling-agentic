## Changelog

**Malaria engine v0.17: the net-type relative effects are drawn engine parameters, and a fit on the pack's training targets is designed (not run).** Up to v0.16 the Churcher 2016 hut-trial coefficients and the decay of PBO's advantage were module constants, so the uncertainty step never drew them. v0.17 adds three parameters (kind `fixed`), all drawn card priors. `net_kill_shift` shifts Churcher's hut-mortality intercept α1 for every net type; it is drawn over the paper's 95% CrI for α1 (0.012–1.29, Results), re-centred on 0.63. `pbo_gain_scale` scales PBO's gain in bioassay-mortality logit (Equation 4); it is drawn over 0.316–1.0. That range comes from Gleave et al. 2021 (Cochrane): the high-resistance hut mortality RR is 1.84 (1.60–2.11), mapped through Churcher's Equation 2 by a coded rule (V36). The rule gives up to 0.912; the range is extended to the engine value 1.0, which the rule finds overstates the pooled hut trials (a principle-4 finding). `pbo_adv_decay` fades a PBO net's advantage over a same-age pyrethroid net; it is drawn over 0–1.895 per year: LLINEUP's total waning between 6 and 18 months (Staedke et al. 2020, Table 2; delta method) less the engine's own waning of the PBO feeding advantage, over the high-resistance category (V37 applies it). No held-out trial is used. At the defaults the outputs are v0.16's: V31 matches a frozen copy of the v0.16 net code bit for bit, and `restamp_proof.py` proved the engine outputs identical before the evidence was re-stamped. `benchmarks/net_effect_fit.py` is a joint-v1 fit of `net_kill_shift`, `cfp_kill`, `irs_kill` and `cfp_half`. Its targets are only the pack's own fit targets, and any held-out target is refused. Every point is calibrated to its sites' control-arm baselines, by interpolation on a grid in `net_kill_shift`, and the result cache is keyed on the calibration. The fit exports a joint sample of trial-compatible parameter sets, with its hull taken before subsampling. Twelve of them are re-run; a point off its targets or more than 10% off its control-arm baseline, a disproved chosen point or a clamped calibration grid invalidates the sample. It needs 3,986 arm runs, priced as allocated VM time at about $11.7–24.1 on one 16-vCPU VM, launched through vm_job.py with --cap-usd 25 after merge. B7/B8 at v0.16: B7 0.759 is in band, and B8 0.665 is out on the side of too little effect. The card showed v0.14's 0.50/0.41 and is regenerated. In four probed configurations none put both in band. B7/B8 stay held out, and a per-trial Cochrane setting is proposed. Self-tests written first: V31–V37 (`vector.py`), NE0–NE3 (`selftest_late.py`), F1–F13 (`net_effect_fit.py`).
