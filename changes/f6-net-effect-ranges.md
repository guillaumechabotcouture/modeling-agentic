## Changelog

**Malaria engine v0.17: the net-type relative effects are engine parameters, and a fit on the pack's training targets is designed (not run).** Up to v0.16 the Churcher 2016 hut-trial coefficients and the decay of PBO's advantage were module constants, so the uncertainty step never drew them. v0.17 adds three parameters (kind `fixed`). `net_kill_shift` is a shift on Churcher's hut-mortality intercept α1 for every net type: deterrence, feeding, killing and insecticide decay then follow from the hut mortality through the paper's own equations, so their correlation is kept. `pbo_gain_scale` scales PBO's gain in bioassay-mortality logit (Equation 4). `pbo_adv_decay` fades a PBO net's advantage over a same-age pyrethroid net. At the defaults (0, 1, 0) the outputs are v0.16's: V31 matches a frozen copy of the v0.16 net code bit for bit, and one engine run_batch (every net type, IRS, a past PBO campaign) has identical output digests under the v0.16 and v0.17 trees. The `restamp_proof.py` proof and the re-stamp of the evidence are pending. `priors.yaml` draws `net_kill_shift` over Churcher 2016's 95% CrI for α1 (0.012–1.29, Results), re-centred on 0.63. The two PBO parameters are fixed: no published range, and no training target has a PBO net. `benchmarks/net_effect_fit.py` is a joint-v1 fit of `net_kill_shift`, `cfp_kill` and `irs_kill`. Its targets are only the pack's own fit targets (B10, B11, B12, B12b, B12m), and it refuses any held-out one. It exports a joint sample of trial-compatible parameter sets, with 20 re-run on the engine. It needs 4,052 arm runs, about $11–22, and refuses to run without the owner's approval; a local smoke ran. Making B9/B9b/B9c training targets with a replacement PBO validation set is proposed, not made. B7/B8 at v0.16: B7 0.759 is in band, and B8 0.665 is out on the side of too little effect. The card's rows still showed v0.14 (0.50, 0.41); v0.17 rewrites them. A local probe shows that no net-use change brings both into band: the trials cut incidence far more than prevalence (0.55 against 0.83), and the engine does not. B7/B8 stay held out, and a per-trial Cochrane setting is proposed (ENGINE_NOTES.md, v0.17). Self-tests written first: V31–V35 (`vector.py`), NE0–NE3 (`selftest_late.py`), F1–F8 (`net_effect_fit.py`).
