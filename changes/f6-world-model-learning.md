## Changelog

**The world model learns only from evidence it has not seen and could not have fitted (F6 WM-L, follow-up to F6 WM).** Every direction (package × feature) and flip (pair × feature) in the summary of `toolkit/analysis/world_model.py` is one test in a Benjamini-Hochberg false-discovery control (q 0.05; the number tested and discovered is reported). Only discoveries become simulation beliefs (`screened: true`). They are labelled as marginal associations with the design features each is correlated with (|rho| ≥ 0.5). A hypothesis claims a conditional mechanism by default, and a correlated feature makes its direction or flip test inconclusive rather than supported or refuted. Each confrontation is identified by the sha256 of its test and of its evidence: the sweep tables, or the plan. Replays and aliases count once and move no confidence, and counters are recomputed from the history (`reconciled` records a difference from the seed's). A hypothesis counts only when its `registered_at` precedes the evidence (the analysis manifest's `finished_at`, or `point.yaml`'s `written_at`). Otherwise it is `post_hoc`, reported apart and never in the share confirmed. A produced world model can seed the next build: its simulation beliefs are superseded (`version`, `superseded_simulation`), literature and data beliefs kept, and hypotheses reloaded with `--hypotheses`. Ids starting `sim.` are reserved. In `--engine-identity recorded` mode the run's engine identity must appear in the engine's benchmark history: `benchmarks/results.yaml` or `history/*`, or `results.yaml` in a commit that is an ancestor of HEAD. Where it matched is recorded, else the build is refused. It also fixes the F6 WM review's round-2 points. The shadow price is no longer inferred from average benefit / cost: it is `--shadow-price`, the budget row's dual recorded in `point.yaml`, or 0 only when the plan records a slack budget; otherwise paired tests are untestable. `point.yaml` is used only when its spec sha256, engine identity and recorded inputs match the run. Verdicts compare full-precision values, and numbers are rounded only on output. Numeric filters read CSV text as numbers and fail closed on a non-number. An equivalence claim is judged on its bound alone. Inputs where one value holds more than half the points give no direction. A belief's conditions are applied to its hypotheses' checks and to the relation check. The units table, the settings file and the engine choice are fingerprinted. Every numeric region must be an exact, finite, ordered pair. `plan_share` tests a component by package composition, or a named package. After review round 1 of this PR, registration is a separate, append-only, hash-chained ledger (`world_model.py register <seed> <run_dir>`, `analysis/hypotheses_registry.jsonl`). No file on a machine the same actor controls can prove when a registration happened, so the claim is narrow. A hypothesis is "remote-anchored" only when its canonical test (defaults resolved, numbers as floats, belief conditions applied, irrelevant or unknown fields refused) is in a ledger line covered by a ledger commit reachable from a fetched remote-tracking ref (`--anchor-ref <remote>/<branch>`, resolved to `refs/remotes/...`; HEAD, local branches and commit ids are refused) whose commit time is before its evidence. That anchor is recorded as "anchored in remote git (commit <sha>); the commit time is the committer's clock". Everything else is post hoc, and the output states that the track record is not tamper-proof. A ledger that does not extend every such head is refused, and `run_state.json` heads are not anchors. Registration holds one lock across read, build and append. The point plan's inputs must verify transitively down the artifact graph and must not be empty (`--without-plan` builds without it). Directions on fewer than 50 points, and flips on fewer than 50 points or with fewer than 10 winners, use permutation p-values (20,000 resamples); every attempted test enters the false-discovery family, a failed fit at p = 1. The point plan is used only when the completed analysis manifest lists it with the current batch and the current analysis settings, and every descendant artifact carries the plan's spec and engine. A test and its mirror (a paired test with its pair and sign reversed, a flip test with its pair reversed) and category lists in any order are one test, a flip belief's location narrows its flip test's range, and a flip not computed for want of a shadow price enters the false-discovery family at p = 1. Benchmark matching compares the full engine identity, provenance requires real digests and recorded inputs for every harness artifact, and repeated belief conditions intersect. The track record was provisional until F6 WM-I added evidence identity, first-availability time and verified history. Self-test WM35-59 and WM67-87, written first.
