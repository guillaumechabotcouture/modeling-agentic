## Changelog

**The world model, v0: beliefs, hypotheses, a coarse sweep summary, check and where to look (F6 WM, design v2-progressive-steps.md §4).** A standalone module, `toolkit/analysis/world_model.py`, not wired into `run.py` or the stage machine. Its simulation-derived beliefs are marginal associations, unscreened for multiple testing (`screened: false`), and must not seed or update a world model until the follow-up (multiplicity control, marginal labels, track-record identity, pre-registration) lands. A seed file (YAML, written by a person now and by a bounded reasoning task later) holds beliefs and hypotheses. A belief has a mechanism and direction, a magnitude band, conditions, its source type (literature, data, simulation) with evidence (citation and table or page; dossier entry; artifact and point ids), a confidence and conflicts. A hypothesis is a testable expectation with a machine-checkable refutation test that declares its pass threshold (`effect_band` coverage, `effect_direction`, `paired_sign`, `flip_in`, `plan_share`) and that must concern its belief's package or pair, feature and conditions. Validation fails closed on a belief without evidence, a hypothesis without a test or threshold, a threshold outside its range, a non-finite value or an unrelated test. `build <run_dir> --seed` reads a run's batch offline and binds every result row by `job_sha` to the stored design's job and to the expected engine identity: the current engine's, or with `--engine-identity recorded` the run's own, for a retro-check. It works per person and compares options on net benefit at the budget's shadow price (`--shadow-price`, else the cost-effectiveness of the point plan's marginal funded package). The outcomes are three-valued. Directions come from a tie-aware Spearman rho with its interval; "negligible" only within a declared equivalence bound; too few usable points, constant or categorical inputs give `inconclusive`. Ties are indifferent. `flip_in` is supported only when the located transition lies inside the claimed range, refitted on its `where` points and widened to the nearest sampled points; a one-sided, rare or non-monotone regime is inconclusive, never refuted. `plan_share` is untestable when matching units or filter columns are missing. Conflicts compare matching conditions and scales, and `where_to_look()` always lists contradicted decision-relevant beliefs. Self-test WM1-34 on planted truths and the review's reproductions.
