## Changelog

**Calibration error model, part 1 (F6 S2-3, C3).** A unit target with `bias_by: <units column>` in `calibration.joint.targets` has one latent offset per survey and group, b ~ N(0, bias_sd²). Before, bias_sd² was added to every target's own variance. `bias_source` names the survey (default: the target id), so two targets of one survey share the offset; a survey declared with two bias sds or two grouping columns is refused. The offset is profiled in `toolkit/calibration/history_matching.py` the way the unit parameter is: a shared value is compatible with a group when one offset with |b| / sd within the cutoff leaves every unit matchable. A coarse pass over `bias_grid` cells allows half a cell as slack (an outer bound). The `bias_cells` most probable feasible cells are then refined on `bias_refine` points per axis with no slack, so the witness is one common offset at which every unit holds exactly. A group target whose units all lie in one component is checked inside the profile. Blocks linked through units are profiled jointly, at most `bias_blocks_max` of them. The engine verification solves the common offset exactly from the engine's runs. The region sample draws each group's offset among its feasible cells (prior-weighted, `bias_tries` proposals per draw), then each unit's parameter given it. The report gives each block's offset at the best point (`bias`). The best point keeps the per-unit score unless a spec chooses `calibration.joint.best_point_weighting: per_group` (each group once, each offset once). A replay of the toy legacy specs on `origin/v2` and on the branch gives identical reports, verification points and sample ids. Thresholds are in `toolkit/calibration/thresholds.yaml`. Tests: HM26 covers four groups with planted survey offsets. With independent errors, a value the within-group contrast rules out stays compatible and the region is wider; with shared offsets the truth is kept and that value is refused. HM27: per_group puts the best point between a 10-unit and a 2-unit group (0.51); the default keeps the per-unit 0.39. HM31: draws cover every compatible unit value, and the group mean varies as one unit does. HM32: a unit survey at 0.5 and an aggregate at 0.7 are fitted together. HM33: two units needing offsets 0.14 and -0.14 conflict, in the profile and the engine verification. HM29, HM30, JA4d and JA15: refusals, the one-block path, the legacy default and the spec adapter. The change between surveys by group follows in S2-3b. Part 2 (historical and future coverage as separate parameters, histories anchored to delivery surveys) needs an engine parameter for past coverage and follows separately.
