## Changelog

**Calibration error model, part 1 (F6 S2-3, C3).** A unit target with `bias_by: <units column>` in `calibration.joint.targets` has one latent offset per survey and group, b ~ N(0, bias_sd²). Before, bias_sd² was added to every target's own variance. `bias_source` names the survey (default: the target id), so two targets of one survey share the offset. At most one survey per unit may carry an offset; a second, or one survey with two bias sds or two grouping columns, is refused. In `toolkit/calibration/history_matching.py` each unit's feasible offsets at a candidate are exact: one closed interval per grid value of its parameter, within ±3 bias_sd. A block's set is the intersection of its units' unions (a sweep). Components join units, their blocks and every member of every group target transitively, so a state aggregate and the state's offset, an aggregate over several groups, or group targets sharing a member are handled together, and each unit is drawn once. Group-target checks include the members' run noise. Offsets are drawn from the prior restricted to each block's exact set and kept when the component's group targets are reachable, so kept draws are draws from the prior restricted to the feasible set. Proposals start at `bias_draws` (64) and double while none is kept, up to `bias_draws_max` (1,024). Only then is a component infeasible at a candidate, recorded with the count reached (`bias_components`). The region sample draws a fresh offset per row, then its units given it, redrawn together until the group targets hold. Values in a component with group targets are kept as drawn (no jitter), and its verification point is one such joint draw. No fixed witness remains anywhere. A row that never holds, or an acceptance rate in the region below `bias_accept_min`, blocks (`offset_sampling_low_acceptance`). The engine verification solves the common offset exactly from its runs. The report gives each block's offset draws at the best point (`bias`). The best point keeps the per-unit score unless a spec chooses `calibration.joint.best_point_weighting: per_group`. A replay of the toy legacy specs on `origin/v2` and on the branch gives identical reports, verification points and sample ids. Thresholds are in `toolkit/calibration/thresholds.yaml`. Tests: HM26 covers four groups with one offset survey per unit, where independent errors keep a value the within-group contrast rules out. HM27 covers per_group and the per-unit default. HM31 checks support, the group mean's variance against the restricted prior's (no aggregate, a weak aggregate, a sampling sd of 1e-5) and no point mass. HM32 covers an aggregate in one group and over two groups. In HM33, units with no common offset conflict, and offsets fitting on [0.134, 0.140] are found. In HM34, a feasible region of about 0.4% of the prior is found by doubling the proposals, and an unreachable aggregate is infeasible at the maximum, with the count. HM35 to HM38 check that the returned rows meet a tight aggregate, that two group targets sharing a member form one component whose rows meet both, that run noise is counted alike in profiling, sampling and verification, and that the verification point is coherent. HM29, HM30, JA4d and JA15 cover refusals, the one-block path, the legacy default and the spec adapter. The change between surveys by group follows in S2-3b. Part 2 (historical and future coverage as separate parameters, histories anchored to delivery surveys) needs an engine parameter for past coverage and follows separately.
