## Changelog

**Malaria engine v0.4: B7 measured in the trials' own age bands, near-edge calibration flag (one pack run, 15 of 17 unchanged).** Written before any run, the scope (ENGINE_NOTES.md, "v0.4 scope") lists eight candidate mechanisms for the remaining gaps, each with its published evidence and expected direction. It commits to one: B7 measured in each identified trial's age band. Five of Pryce 2018's six prevalence trials were identified: 0–59 months, 15 and under, all ages twice, under 10. Sochantha 2006 shows the age dependence: all-age prevalence ratio 0.91 against an under-5 OR of 0.63, age interaction p = 0.042. The engine reports microscopy prevalence in those bands (`model.PFPR_BANDS`), and `benchmark.py` gains a ratio over several weighted value paths (`values`, with `components` and `diagnostic_values` reported). B7 is an equal-weight geometric mean over the five trials; the Cochrane weights are not accessible, so the weights are an assumption. The runner also flags a calibrated value within 5% (log) of an outer grid bound (`near_grid_edge`), and Muleba's grid extends to 256 (v0.3: m0 127.6 against a bound of 128). Tests came first (engine M19–M21, runner BP31–BP36, renderer R1). The pack was fitted and run once on a 32-CPU Coiled VM (IDM default workspace). B7 0.589 (v0.3 0.595), out of band. The engine's net effect is 0.56 in under-5s against 0.60 in all ages, while Sochantha reported 0.63 against 0.91. The engine cuts adult prevalence nearly as much as child prevalence, so the remaining gap points at the human module, not the outcome definition. B8 0.462, still marginal; B16c 4 of 5 rules, with no mechanism committed (Beier 1999 full text not accessible). Fitted values are unchanged (cfp_kill 0.425, irs_kill 0.4216), and no setting is near a grid edge. v0.3 results, fitted values and scans are kept in `benchmarks/history/`. `contract.py` is unchanged.
