## Changelog

**Sensitivity wiring: every declared input must reach a declared output.** New `toolkit/analysis/wiring.py` (owner, 2026-11-23, after an outside benchmark where a hard-coded constant silently disabled a declared sensitivity scenario). After the decision tests, every input the signed spec declares as varying (fitted, prior, scenario and calibrated engine parameters with their ranges, the sensitivity list, the parameter of each parameter_value / prior_range / fitted_range test, sensitivity and decision-test cost items, burden anchor groups) is moved to each end of its range with the others at their point values; other model-input decision tests (unit attributes, interactions, ratios, targets) are read from their own records. Each must move at least one declared output (each outcome under each package, each package's cost; sum over units of the absolute change, relative to the point) by more than its tolerance (`thresholds.yaml wiring`: `rel_tol`; on direct evaluation `noise_multiplier` times the change one seed shift makes at the point, zero for a deterministic engine; `emulator_rel_tol` on the emulator path). An input that moves nothing, moves less than the noise, or has no range is an `input_not_wired` finding (`contract.gate_findings`, target stage MODEL). The check is ADVISORY (lead, after Codex round 2): the finding is MEDIUM, scope-declarable and marked `would_block: true`, a check that fails to run is recorded rather than raised, and nothing fails a run; it is made blocking once real runs show its false-positive rate. On the emulator path only a change above `emulator_wired_rel` certifies an input (`emulator_unverified` below it); identity ranges are `not_perturbed`, inputs acting only through pruned packages `not_applicable`, conditional tests keep their `with` settings, the noise floor is per output, and `uncertainty.sources` cost ranges get rows. Exemptions: `analysis.wiring_exempt: {parameters | cost_items | burden_anchors | decision_tests: {<name>: <reason>}}` (validated by the schema, documented in the decision-spec skill); results.md lists every exemption, unused ones too. The sensitivity ends and decision-test evaluations are recorded as they run (`Recorder`) and reused; extra evaluations (at most two per input plus one noise probe) and engine runs are counted in `analysis/wiring.yaml`, which the manifest now expects. No batch job is added or changed, so job hashes and the reuse key are unchanged. Self-tests WR1-WR21, R-WR1-R-WR3.
