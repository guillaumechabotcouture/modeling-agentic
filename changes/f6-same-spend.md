## Changelog

**A fixed plan compared with the optimum at its own cost (F6 S2-6c; project review finding A2).** A `fixed_plan` variant's `value_lost` compares it with the unconstrained plan solved within the headline plan's budget (the reserve when enforced), so it mixes a different allocation with a different spend. Each evaluated fixed plan's record in `analysis/plan_variants.yaml` now also carries `same_spend`: the optimum re-solved on the same point tables and the unconstrained (signed) feasibility with the budget set to the fixed plan's own point cost, guarded against the point model corrections as every other solve. It records `budget`, `objective`, `cost`, `value_gain` (its outcomes averted minus the fixed plan's), `value_gain_pct` (as a share of its outcomes averted), the move summary from the given plan to it, and `plan`; when no plan is feasible at that spend it is `status: not_solved` with the reason, and any other solver error stops the analysis. `value_lost` is unchanged. The plan-variants contract requires the block for every evaluated fixed plan and checks it against the record (budget equals the plan's cost, the optimum within it, value gain and its share recomputed). `results.md` states the equal-spend comparison for each fixed plan, with the claims `variant.<id>.same_spend_gain` and `same_spend_gain_pct`. The comparison is at the point estimate; the same-spend plan is not followed over the uncertainty draws. Self-tests PV37–PV40 (`variants.py`, PV37 against a brute force over the toy's packages) and PV-R14 (`run.py`). Codex round 1: `value_gain` is now in undiscounted primary outcomes averted (from the point outcomes; `averted` and `given_averted` beside it) and the objective difference is kept apart as `objective_gain`; a not-solved block carries the plan's cost as `budget` and is a contract finding when the fixed plan is itself feasible under the signed constraints; the contract checks that a solved block's plan gives each of the fixed plan's units a package id and that every number is finite, reporting a bad field as a finding, never an exception; results.md says the spending is capped at the plan's cost (it may spend less) and that a plan breaking the signed constraints can give a negative gain (PV41–PV45, including a variant headline that must not change the same-spend feasibility and a model correction on the same-spend plan alone that stops the analysis).
