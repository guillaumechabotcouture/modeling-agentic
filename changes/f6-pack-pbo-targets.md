## Changelog

**Malaria pack: LLINEUP and Misungwi dual-vs-PBO trial targets (option A, step 1).** `toolkit/engines/malaria/benchmarks/pack.yaml` gains B9d, B9e and B9f, Staedke 2020 (LLINEUP, Uganda, 104 clusters), Table 2 as-treated, PBO vs non-PBO LLIN prevalence ratio by microscopy in children 2-10 years at 6, 12 and 18 months (0.74, 0.73, 0.84; bands are the published 95% CIs), on a new `llineup` setting (arms `ug_pyr`, `ug_pbo`) written from the trial description as an assumption (bioassay mortality 0.15, species mix, bimodal seasonality, one distribution date) and calibrated to the trial's non-PBO arm at 6 months (556/3844 = 0.145); its median net retention (1088.1 d) reproduces the trial's reported use, 85% at 6 months and 73% at 18. B9g is Mosha 2022's dual-AI vs PBO odds ratio at 24 months on the existing Misungwi arms (target 0.455 = B10 / B9c), with a `band_override` (0.26-0.793, level 0.95) derived by the delta method from the two published intervals taken as independent, since the trial publishes no direct interval. All four are held out; `benchmark_coverage.yaml` maps them (PBO; dual-AI against PBO). The estimand types already existed (`ratio` with source `risk_ratio`; `odds_ratio`). `benchmark_sources.load_evidence` now names a pack benchmark with no stored row `not yet run` apart from a stale row (`rerun required`); both refuse the record, so until the pack is re-run the malaria engine's verified benchmark record is refused (fail closed). New module `toolkit/engines/malaria/benchmarks/pack_trials.py` (`or_contrast`, `retention_median`) checks the derivations without an engine run. `net_effect_fit.load_wave1` binds wave-1 reuse to the fit's declarations (training targets and fit rule, `WAVE1_FIT_DECLARATIONS_SHA256`) instead of the whole pack file, so held-out additions leave it reusable. Self-tests PT0-PT6, BS68-68b, F19c.
