## Changelog

**Compartmental engine v0.3: the direct final-size solver.** A `final_size: true` run with `forcing: none`, no component acting during the epidemic and `importation: deterministic` solves the multigroup final-size relation z = s - (s - e) exp(-q C z) directly instead of stepping the Starsim sim (`solve_final_size`, `infection_reach` in `toolkit/engines/compartmental/engine.py`; plan `docs/design/direct-final-size-solver.md`, PR #346). Reach follows the direction of infection after the interventions, through groups with susceptibles left. Lower and upper iterates start at e and s and stay sub- and super-solutions: plain steps move by T less or plus a bound on its rounding error, and Newton candidates are kept group by group only where the check holds against that bound (Codex r1), so rounding never certifies a bound; the rounding bound carries an absolute allowance for underflow, inputs are promoted to binary64, zero rows are exact and an overflowing force saturates (Codex r2). The map is evaluated in the expm1 form. A member iterates at least once, stops when its bracket is at most `tol` wide in every group, and returns the lower bound. A bracket still open at `step_cap` iterations, or whose bounds stop moving for 50 iterations, fails closed. `meta.steps` counts solver iterations per member, `meta.time_axis` is `solver_iterations`, and `meta.final_size_path` records the path on every final_size run. The engine does not import `benchmarks/oracles.py`. Every other configuration keeps the sim, with per-capita outcomes, windows and cost drivers byte-identical. Engine 0.2 to 0.3 (new code sha256, so row reuse keys move). `results.yaml` was re-run: 62 of 62 assessable in band, with `age5_final` tol 1e-14 to 1e-12, the solver's certified limit near criticality. The card was regenerated. `compat_proof.yaml` was re-proved against the v0.1 commit that computed the coverage rows (27 of 27 identical), and `coverage_results.yaml` was re-stamped. No coverage configuration sets `final_size`, so there was no VM re-run. On the flu R2 spec, with 3,374 packages on one draw, run locally: about 0.5 ms per member for the whole run_batch, against about 50-65 ms per member stepping. The solver itself takes about 12 µs per member; the p_major diagnostic dominates. Self-tests FD1-FD22 (`selftest_final_size.py`).
