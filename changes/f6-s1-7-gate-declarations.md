## Changelog

**Gate and declarations (F6 S1-7).** Declared findings keep their full claim and required remedy: the v2 machine cut each open finding's claim at 300 characters (`agents/stage_machine.py:open_findings`) and never carried the critic's `fix_requires`, so `declared_findings.yaml` and the report's open-findings list ended mid-sentence with no remedy. Each declared entry now has a separate short `label` beside the full `claim`, `required_remedy` and `tested`; the report lists each in full with its remedy, and renders every normalised `scope_declaration.yaml` entry (all its text fields, any shape the gate reads) with its remedy. The ledger (`findings.jsonl`, now with `fix_requires`), the fix planner's packet (full claim and remedy beside a short label; task briefs use the label), the owner's scope sign-off items, the done_when registry, the gate's `unresolved_high` (now with `fix_requires`) and the persistence escalation's quoted claim no longer truncate. A done_when test whose result is null is reported as `not_judged` (its detail opens "not judged: …", not "pass …"), and the gate records `check_summary` (passed, failed, not judged, withdrawn; `required_not_judged`). A required test that is not judged cannot yield ACCEPT: in the v2 machine the fix loop (4m) and rule 3m handle such a finding first, and rule 3j declares any that still reaches ACCEPT, after the structural, owner and non-declarable rules; the STAGE 7 validator adds an accept blocker. `report.md` opens with a harness status line ("Run status: declared scope, not accepted." for `completed_scope_declared`), re-rendered at every post-write gate; `report_render.json` records the writer's span with the report's hash. Acceptance language ("accepted", "validated", "conclusively", "definitive", "proven", "passed the gate", "approved plan") is listed in `.claude/orchestration/acceptance_language.yaml`, with quoted, type-checked negations that count only in the term's own clause, allowed owner phrases and attributed critic quotations. `scripts/writer_qa.py` takes the run's status from `final_gate.json` and `run_state.json` only (never `metadata.json`; conflicting records count as not accepted) and skips only the harness blocks `report_render.json` authenticates. Before a report the final gate did not accept is delivered, the machine checks the whole manuscript: acceptance language goes back to the writer (at most two repairs, re-rendered and re-checked), and a report that keeps it is withheld (`report_withheld.md`, `completed_no_report`); `final_gate.json` records what was found and what remains. Retro-check on the local measles run 2026-10-20_213310: 31 open findings, 20 with claims over 300 characters (cut before), all 31 kept in full with their remedies; on the accepted malaria run 2026-05-03_235631 the check finds 3 uses of "validated" that a declared-scope report could not contain.
