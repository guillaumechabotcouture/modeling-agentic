## Changelog

**Net-effect fit acceptance rule: one seed value per declared seed, and the interval on the geometric mean.** Codex's review of PR #233 (P2) found that `point_in_band`, the noise-aware acceptance rule shared by `finalize()` and `load_posterior`'s replay, accepted any per-seed list of 2 or more values without checking it against the run's seeds. A B11 list truncated to [0.2, 0.4] passed, while the full eight values ([0.2, 0.4] + [0.2]×6) failed. The rule now requires exactly one finite, positive value per declared seed. The seeds come from `pack.yaml` (the default, and the loader's replay) or from the run's own seeds (`run()`). A missing, extra or duplicate seed, or a seed declaration that is missing or not distinct integers, fails closed, and `finalize()` names the mismatch. `load_posterior` also refuses a file whose recorded `fit_rule.seeds` are not `pack.yaml`'s. The rule's 95% interval is now taken on the log scale around the geometric mean of the seed values, exp(mean(log v) ± 1.96 sd(log v)/√n). That is the same statistic as each target's `computed` value, where the rule used the arithmetic mean before. The output records the statistic (`verified.acceptance_statistic`) and the seeds it checked against (`verified.seeds`). The accepted round-2 output (sha256 d45eebb2…64aa) was replayed through `load_posterior` locally, with no engine run, and still passes: 12 of 12 verified points and the chosen point are in band, and the closest margin is the chosen point's B10 interval, which starts 0.0175 below the band's upper edge of 0.67. Self-tests F14–F15 (3 new loader refusal cases, 26 in all), F26 (the reviewer's probe, plus 8 seed-declaration cases) and F27 (the log-scale interval, a skewed seed set, and zero or negative values) were written first and failed before the change (α). F11 and F24 were updated to the new signature (β).
