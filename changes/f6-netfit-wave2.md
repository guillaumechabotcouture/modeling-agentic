## Changelog

**Net-effect fit, wave 2: a wider `cfp_half` box, wave 1's engine results reused, a second design wave where wave 1 accepted, and an optional margin on the exported sample.** Wave 1 (cluster 2375765, $15.18) verified its chosen point but invalidated its joint sample, with 8 of 12 verified points in band. Its four misses were narrow, at B12b's lower bound and B12m's upper bound. Its accepted `cfp_half` hull reached the box's lower edge. The `cfp_half` design box goes from 547.5–1,277.5 to 270–1,650 days. Its ends are exponential half-lives we derived from two Interceptor G2 durability studies, both opened: the lower end from chlorfenapyr content (Ngufor 2024, Parasites & Vectors 17:420, Table 8; Fagbohoun 2026, PLOS Global Public Health) and the upper end from tunnel-test and hut mortality (Ngufor 2024 Table 7; Fagbohoun 2026). It is a design box, not a prior. `priors.yaml` keeps `cfp_half` under `fixed`, with a note, so `load_posterior`'s card-range check is unchanged and does not apply to it. Wave 1's output is kept verbatim as `benchmarks/net_effect_fit_wave1.yaml`. `load_wave1` reuses its 59 engine-evaluated points (46 design, 12 verified, 1 chosen) and its calibration grid. It fails closed unless the file's sha256, the full engine identity, `pack.yaml`'s sha256, the seeds, the targets and the calibration grid all match (F19, 10 refusal cases). `run` adds 48 LHS points in wave 1's accepted hull, widened by 25% of its width on each side, or to the new box edge on a side where the hull reached the old edge (`wave2_box`, F20). It refits the quadratic surrogates on both waves under rule `joint-v1-wave2` (`fit_waves`, F21). Only the new points, the chosen point and the 12 verified points run (3,904 arm runs, no calibration runs). `finalize()` and the acceptance gate are unchanged, and so is `load_posterior`. `--margin-loo` (off by default; on for this launch, owner's choice) exports only candidates that the surrogate puts inside each band by that target's leave-one-out error over the points in the wave-2 box. The output records the margin, the share of the in-band region it excludes and the excluded candidates' ranges under `joint_region.margin`. That narrowing is a declared limitation (F22). `plan` reports `usd_real` at the measured $0.124 per vCPU-hour beside the VM price bound. `run` refuses a plan above $25 on either basis: $11.0–22.5 and $12.5–18.4 for this one, on 16 vCPUs. Self-tests F2, F4, F13 and F19–F22 were written first and failed before the change (α).
