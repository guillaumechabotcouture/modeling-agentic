## Changelog

**The solver gap, and auxiliary solves that degrade (v2 autonomy brief, PR 1C item 3, decision D3).** `toolkit/analysis/thresholds.yaml` gains an `optimizer` block: relative gap, threads above a unit count, time limit, the gap an auxiliary solve may accept, the share of unusable auxiliary solves above which the gate reports. `toolkit/alloc/optimize.py` `solve()` takes `gap_rel` and `threads` (default exact and single-threaded, as the benchmark stays), records both, and gives a solve that stopped early its proven gap to the LP relaxation's bound. `core.Analysis.solve` reads the settings: a headline solve (the point plan, the reserve search, a rebuild) must be proven optimal within the gap; inside `core.auxiliary()` a stopped solve within the accepted gap is used and counted; anything else raises `SolveFailed` with the solver's status. A proven result is kept for the run (an accepted feasible one for auxiliary solves only), so one problem solved twice returns one plan (the variants' second solve, audit 3.10). Uncertainty draws, sensitivity ends, value of information, stability folds, decision tests, plan variants and the frontier are auxiliary: a fold or decision test with no usable plan is `not_run` and a frontier envelope `not_solved`, each with the status; a proven infeasibility is handled as before, and the variants' same-spend optimum stays a strict solve. This reverses two behaviours set on purpose: self-test R5n6 (a non-infeasible solver failure stopped a cost test) and FD16b (it propagated from the frontier). Records: `point.yaml` `solver` carries the gap, threads and time limit; the manifest's `solver` the settings and per-step counts; `results.md` the gap; `contract.py` reports `analysis_solver_degraded` above the share. `figure_data.rebuild` accepts a stored plan that passes the allocator's check and is within the gap of the solver's gain. Where an auxiliary solve accepted an unproven plan, `core.best_known` takes the better of it and the point plan, `moves.solver_gap` records the accepted gap as a bound beside the regret (`regret_solver_gap`), and a decision test whose verdict cannot be settled inside that bound reads `WITHIN_SOLVER_GAP`; both solves of the value of information's `clean_solve` are auxiliary. Self-tests TG1-TG4, SG1-SG10, JR9f-JR9g, R11b, R5n6, KS1-KS5, PV35, FD10b-FD10d, FD16b, MV24-MV26, VC1.
