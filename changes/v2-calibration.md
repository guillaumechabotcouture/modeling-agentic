## Changelog

**Harness `posterior` calibration, observation sources and identifiability classes (v2d β, calibration part).** `toolkit/analysis/observation.py` gains the observation likelihoods (Poisson, negative binomial with a size, binomial, beta-binomial from a survey's design effect, lognormal reporting noise; invalid inputs fail closed) and the per-source measurement model of §3.4: a source declares what it measures, its sampling frame (design, excluded populations with their share `phi` and a relative-value prior) and its bias mechanisms from a closed vocabulary, each with a parameter, a prior and a source; free or per-unit multipliers, a mechanism without a prior and `bias: none` without a reason are refused (`bias_without_mechanism`, `observation_source_undeclared`, `frame_gap_unmodelled`). `toolkit/analysis/calibration.py`: bounded priors (uniform, log-uniform, beta, triangular), a deterministic sampler chosen by dimension (2^14 scrambled Sobol points over the prior plus adaptive Gaussian stages pooled by the balance heuristic, ESS floor 1,000, up to 4 parameters; 4-chain adaptive Metropolis with rank-normalised split R-hat <= 1.01 and bulk ESS >= 400 above), each failing closed; the canonical joint sample with an id over names and values; identifiability classes from the Fisher information of the likelihood alone in prior-scale units (flat directions, ridge loadings, derived quantities such as a source's net bias) plus contraction on each prior's own scale; automatic `prior_range` tests and the label's prior-dominated names; draws recorded as (sample id, row, applied values) and re-checked against the sample (`analysis_contract_uncovered_parameter`). `likelihood` fits on a ridge now stop with `calibration_ridge` instead of a jittered covariance. The allocation path accepts `calibration.method: posterior` on a count panel (declared priors for every parameter and the unit attribute, a declared observation model): point at the posterior mode, uncertainty from canonical rows with provenance, posterior stability folds, prior_range tests redo the calibration. The coverage pack's SB1-SB3 and SB5 are declared for this calibrator and were run on two dev VMs (200 replicates each; `benchmarks/coverage_results.yaml`). 9 of 10 cases are in band: SB1 0.96, SB2a 0.95, SB2c 0.97, SB3a 0.925, SB3c 0.905 and SB5 0.93, and the negative controls under-cover (SB2a_neg 0.335, SB3b 0.18 and 0.465). In SB3a ω and δ are prior-dominated and the source's net bias is identified in 200/200 replicates; in SB3c every bias parameter is prior-dominated in 200/200. SB2b (seeding, susceptible share and forcing all unknown) is out of band: 143 of 200 fits refused below the ESS floor, and a better proposal is the open item. The renewal SB cases now generate from an independent discrete renewal process (O19), run in daily steps reported weekly. SB5's q range starts above criticality. Two Codex rounds (11 + 5 findings), each fixed test first. VM spend 70.2 credits (about US$4.28). The benchmark runner runs coverage replicates in parallel (equal to serial), counts a refusing calibrator as an invalid replicate, checks `ridge_names`, and merges per-case runs (`run --only`, `merge`). `contract.py` and the malaria engine are unchanged.
