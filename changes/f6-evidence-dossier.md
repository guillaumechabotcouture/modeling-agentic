## Changelog

**No sign-off until every input that drives an option's effect has a checked, sourced evidence entry (owner decision 2026-11-12).** Runs reached paid compute with decision-moving inputs nobody had checked against field data, and the post-hoc gates, which check internal consistency, passed. This is a precondition, not another gate. Each engine card declares its `decision_inputs`: per package component, the unit attributes, engine parameters and card tables its effect depends on, with the values that have no effect (`toolkit/engines/decision_inputs.py`). The declarations live in a `decision_inputs.yaml` beside each card (malaria, compartmental, renewal, package-rate), merged with any `decision_inputs` key in card.yaml. They are kept out of code and out of `contract.py`, whose bytes are in every engine's code fingerprint, so no stamped benchmark result goes stale. A spec names its run's dossier (`evidence: {dossier: evidence_dossier.yaml}`). Each entry gives the model value and its source; then either field or trial observations (source, URL or table, year, sites, n, sampling frame, value) with a comparison, or an explicit `no_data` with its borrowing rule; then the chosen value or range, its rule, and the decision test for a range (`toolkit/spec/evidence_dossier.py`). `toolkit/spec/signoff.py` refuses to sign, whoever signs, when the dossier is missing, unparseable, blank anywhere or missing an input of a component the options use (for `engine: auto`, of every eligible engine), and it lists what is missing. It also refuses when an engine cannot map a component (fail closed). The sheet renders the dossier table, records its sha256 in its sources, and the signature binds it, so an edit after sign-off makes the status `changed`. `cloud/run_cluster.py stale_sheet_problems` refuses a launch whose shipped dossier differs from the one the sheet recorded (at SIGN) or the sign-off recorded (past SIGN). A spec whose options use no component with declared inputs needs no dossier. After review round 1, every signing path (`scripts/decision_brief.py` and `agents/stage_machine.py` too) goes through the same `evidence_dossier.bind`. The inputs the options need decide whether a dossier is required, not whether the spec names one, and a sign-off made before this requirement, of a spec that needs a dossier, is `unsigned_evidence`. Past SIGN, the launch requires the shipped spec to match its signed hash, while SPEC drafts are not checked. ENGINE_CHOICE re-checks the dossier against the engine it picks, and `engine: auto` uses the backend's full eligibility. Malformed declarations and missing `engine_map` entries fail closed. Blank checks recurse into nested values, and text fields must be text. A range's decision test must vary that input over the range. Configured engines resolve their inputs against the configured card (`if_on_card`, globs), and every card parameter and unit attribute is either a declared input or excluded with a reason (`not_decision_inputs`, checked by ED-E3). Review round 2: the brief's signer recomputes the binding and refuses a supplied one that differs, and refuses a dossier changed after the sheet. `prior_range_<p>` counts only for a prior the draw plan draws (`priors.drawn`). A value the spec runs (`engine_parameters` or a unit attribute bound to a value) must match the dossier's model and chosen values, or lie in its chosen range. Outcome-level inputs (`outcome_inputs`) are required whenever the primary or headline outcome uses them, whatever the components: malaria treatment, severe-care fatality and DALY inputs, and compartmental outcome ratios. Review round 3 replaces the per-path checks with one function, `evidence_dossier.state()`, registered in a shared `toolkit/spec/preconditions.py` that every path calls (the three signers, `signoff.status`, launch and relaunch, and the stage machine at ENGINE_CHOICE and on resume). It recomputes everything from the files every time and only compares a stored record, never trusts it. It adds reconciliation against card defaults and structured literals, a chosen range that must contain the draw support, and a decision test that must execute on the run's path (`preconditions.decision_test_runs`). The stage-machine signer and the launch at SIGN now check coverage and the sheet's dossier sha256. A regression table (`toolkit/spec/evidence_regressions.py`) drives every reviewed bypass through every path. Malaria nets now depend on `season` and `net_holes`, and IRS on `season`. Per-component completeness is checked by perturbation (`decision_inputs.dependency_problems`; renewal and compartmental in the self-test). Review round 4: the dossier attests inputs, not outputs. A calibrated parameter or attribute attests its declared range and its calibration targets. A drawn or fitted parameter attests its declared range and cites the test the runner's own generator (`decision_tests.prior_range_tests`) names for it. Values come from execution's functions: `resolve_params` and `batch.fitted_ranges` for parameters, and `unit_attributes` and the binding defaults for attributes (a column's min, median and max, or the per-unit values' sha256). Card tables are matched by content sha256. Values are computed once per call, with no caches. The signed inputs may be a superset of the chosen engine's, and declaration changes are detected by a content hash. The sheet's spec sha256 is checked. `signoff: none` follows one rule in the preflight and at runtime. The launch checks the shipped tree in a child process. The table drives the real `Machine.run` and `relaunch`.
