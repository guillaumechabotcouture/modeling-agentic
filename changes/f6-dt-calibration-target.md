## Changelog

**A `calibration_target` decision test (owner decision R1, F6 run 1).** The owner's R1 declares the Katsina and Yobe calibration misses, each tied to a decision test on those states' allocation with their transmission set from the 2018 survey level. No existing test type could express that. `unit_attribute` refuses the calibration-target column and the calibrated attribute. On the joint path, `parameter_value` on `z:<unit>` is ignored, because the units keep their joint values. The new type, `calibration_target: {units: [ids], scale}` (`toolkit/analysis/decision_tests.py` `_calibration_target`), multiplies the listed units' `calibration.target_column` values by `scale` and redoes their calibrated values on the outcome emulator's calibration metric (`model.cal_grid`, as `calibration.invert`). Every other unit is held, and the plan is re-solved. Without a joint calibration, the new value is the inversion at the scaled target. On the joint path, where the unit values come from the joint fit (offsets, group targets) and a single-target inversion would drop them, each listed unit's joint value moves by the difference between the inversions at the scaled and at the original target. It is not run (fail closed) when: a listed unit is not in the analysis, or, on the joint path, not in the joint calibration; a unit's inversion is clamped at the range at either target; or no unit moves. The record keeps each unit's target, scaled target, value before and after, and both inversions. `toolkit/spec/schema.py` `_calibration_target_problems` refuses at sign-off: anything but an emulator-inversion calibration, a non-empty list of known unit ids without repeats, a positive scale other than 1, and every scaled target inside (0, 1). Self-tests SP-CT1-7 (schema) and JR10/JR10b (`run.py`, joint path: the listed units' values move and the plan is re-solved; a unit the joint calibration did not fit is not run) failed first (the type was unknown).
