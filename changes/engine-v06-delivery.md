## Changelog

**Malaria engine v0.6: a delivery period distinct from the outcome window, and routine net campaigns in every arm (owner decision 2026-11-02, Nigeria v2).** The Nigeria v2 run counted benefit over 2024.0–2027.0 while each net campaign paid its full cost: 67 of 309 units with a 2026.25 campaign were credited with about 0.75 y of a roughly 3-year net life. The new spec field is `objective.delivery: [t0, t1]`. It is optional, lies inside `objective.horizon`, and is allowed only for an engine declaring `supports_delivery`. It reaches engines as the `delivery` field of a `DeliveryHorizon`, a subclass of the contract's `Horizon` in the new `toolkit/engines/horizon.py`; `contract.py` is unchanged, so the compartmental engine's stamps are untouched. The analysis batch writes `delivery` into job horizons only when the spec gives it, so other specs' job hashes do not change. The malaria engine runs SMC rounds, default IRS rounds and the enhanced case-management add-on only in delivery years. With an explicit period, any component event outside it fails closed, and so does a period outside the window. Cost drivers (`nets_given`, `smc_doses`, `irs_sprayed`, `treated_all`) count the delivery period; health outcomes count the outcome window. `treated_all` counts the delivery period because treatment after it is not funded by the decision, and the add-on has stopped by then. The new unit attribute `itn_routine_interval` adds routine campaigns at `campaign_year + k x interval` before the window end. They run in every package and the comparator, use net type `hist_net`, are never charged, and fail closed inside the delivery period. Without the new field and attribute, a run is byte-identical to v0.5. Self-test D9 compares an equal explicit period exactly. `benchmarks/restamp_proof.py` ran the pack's settings and the evidence cases under origin/v2 and v0.6 and found them identical: 19 of 19 cases, the 14 pack settings with all 27 arms at seed 11 and each setting's own n_agents, burn-in, fitted values and calibrated attribute, plus 3 equilibration and 2 guess-sensitivity cases; about 41 CPU-minutes on 2 processes. The identity fields of results.yaml, fitted.yaml, equilibration.yaml and guess_sensitivity.yaml were then re-stamped by that proof (each records `restamped:`). A Coiled validation run (validate_once.sh) is open until it reports. Tests came first: engine D0–D9, spec SP-D1–SP-D3, batch BA-D1, horizon H1–H4, proof RP1–RP6. No cloud compute.
