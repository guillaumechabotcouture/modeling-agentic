## Changelog

**Compartmental engine on Starsim, its benchmark pack and the runner types it needs (v2d δ).** Runtime spike on the dev VM first (one `ss.Module` with the exponential-survival step, 5 groups, one core): 37/56/92 ms per sim at 50/100/200 steps and 54/88/149 ms for a batch of 1,024, inside the note's 20-100 ms estimate, so no emulator fallback. `toolkit/engines/compartmental/engine.py`: one `GroupEpidemic` `ss.Module` holding batch x groups state (no agents, no network), `ss.Intervention` components acting at the start of the sim (campaign or dose blocks, all-or-nothing, doses to immune people wasted unless `targets_susceptible`), groups with a contact matrix or birth cohorts mixing as C_ij = c N_j / N, initialisers `given` and `cohort_history` (birth-year indexed, independent or nested doses), `to_extinction` (doubling segments, early stop, fail closed at the step cap; growth judged on the groups the seeds can reach) and `windowed` modes, `run_batch()` (one unit or one per pair) and `initial_state()`, `p_major` from the multitype extinction equations labelled derived, and `card.yaml` with capabilities by configuration and the results sha256. The benchmark runner gains estimand types `value`, `identity` (number / arm path / oracle / probe sides; eq, le, differ; vector paths) and `coverage` (generator, calibrator, replicates; a missing calibrator is `not_assessable`), and pack `configs` carried through worker jobs, the cache key and `configs_validated`. Test oracles (Lambert W, herd immunity, final-size fixed point, rank-one reduction, Wallinga-Lipsitch, branching identities, a multigroup SIR ODE) live in `benchmarks/oracles.py` and are never exposed to runs. `toolkit/analysis/observation.py` holds only the bias-mechanism forward maps. Pack: 51 of 51 assessable benchmarks in band (FS1-FS8, BR1, RN1-RN4 with the linear-form negative control, CI1-CI4, OB1), FS7 and RN5 not_assessable until transcribed. SB1-SB3 and SB5 are written with independent generators but are not_assessable until workstream β provides the `posterior` calibration. `contract.py` and the malaria engine are unchanged. VM spend about 2.3 Coiled credits (~US$0.14).
