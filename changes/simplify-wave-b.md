## Changelog

**Wave B (B1–B3): cut brittle `v2` prose/regex rules, simplify `stage_machine.py` & `PathPolicy`, and delete the non-gating `world_model` side-channel.**

- **B1 — Brittle `v2` rules, redundant advisories & legacy `batch`/`figure_data` cuts:** Delete `.claude/orchestration/acceptance_language.yaml` and remove the acceptance-language withholding/repair loop (`agents/stage_machine.py`, `agents/v2_sdk_tasks.py`, `agents/v2_tasks.py`) while inlining `rename_nofollow` into `_withhold_report()` and `_STATUS_LINES` into `_status_line()` so deterministic `**Run status: ...**` banners remain unchanged. Cut `typed_numbers()`, `given_values()`, `report_number_typed`, and `_repair_claim_refs` / `_recover_claim_repair` / `_claim_repair_undo` while keeping `[CLAIM:id]` rendering and structural `report_claim_*` checks. Remove 5 brittle spec/dossier/requested regex rules (no-digits-in-prose, `EXPECTED_OBJECTIVE_RE`, held-out id prose grep with `EP55` aligned to structured `basis="fitted"`, `observation_acquired_unbound`, and `check_plan_summary()`), delete 5 redundant advisory modules (`toolkit/spec/review_conditions.py`, `toolkit/analysis/wiring.py`, `toolkit/spec/critique_checklist.py`, `toolkit/analysis/red_flag.py`, `toolkit/spec/evidence_regressions.py`), and remove legacy `batch.py` `adopt`/`ship`/`merge_shipped`/`load_shipped` and `figure_data.py` `rebuild`/`ulp_match`/`plan_within_gap`/`derived_fields`.
- **B2 — State-machine simplification, 4-module split & unified `PathPolicy`:** Remove the lead provisional spec-amendment fork, child handoff chain, and parent-child storage chain (`_amend_lock`, `_lead_amend*`, `handoff_child`, `handoff_chain`, `_store_chain`, `for_run_dir`), replacing built-engine single-process concurrency (`BE31`) with narrow `_run_lock` / `RunLocked` (`analysis/run.lock`) and pausing signed-spec rethink in `h_SPEC` via `AWAITING_SPEC_AMENDMENT`. Remove `CONFIGURE` and `PRESENTATION` states (folding backend validation and `_write_pipeline` into `h_ANALYZE` and presentation critique inline at the end of `h_WRITE`), delete `_rereview_due`, `SIGN` auto-repair, and `stamp_critique` (`CS1`-`CS12`). Replace the 7-layer `v2_gate` stack and `harness_gaps` with `agents/v2_gate.py` while preserving mechanical `done_when` tests and `answer_fingerprint`. Split `agents/stage_machine.py` along an acyclic DAG (`v2_gate.py` -> `report_render.py` -> `engine_build_stage.py` -> `stage_machine.py`) with re-exports and `scripts/self_tests.py` entries, unify read/write isolation hooks in `agents/v2_sdk_tasks.py` under `PathPolicy` (with `Glob`/`Grep` `..` traversal denial), harden `report_render.deliverable_problems()` and `_write_deliverable()`, and update `main.py`, `agents/run_finish.py`, `tools/run_console/`, and `tools/workbench/fixtures.py`.
- **B3 — Delete non-gating `world_model` (`PREREGISTER`/`SCORE`) side-channel:** Delete `toolkit/analysis/world_model.py`, `toolkit/analysis/belief_table.py`, `agents/world_model_stage.py`, `scripts/decision_beliefs.py`, `scripts/world_model_trials.py`, `scripts/world_model_trials_probe.py`, `scripts/world_model_trials_questions.yaml`, and all 286 tracked files under `data/world_model_trials/`. Remove `PREREGISTER` and `SCORE` from `agents/stage_machine.py` (`STATES` reduced to 16), `agents/engine_build_stage.py`, `agents/report_render.py`, `agents/v2_sdk_tasks.py` (`seed_world_model`, `PathPolicy.seed_read_denied`, `SW1`-`SW3`), `agents/__init__.py`, `toolkit/spec/amend.py`, `toolkit/analysis/batch.py`, `scripts/compute.py`, `scripts/self_tests.py`, and `.gitignore`.
