## Changelog

**Harness metrics: completion, owner interventions and owner-wait per run chain.** New read-only `scripts/harness_metrics.py <run_dir>... [--chain a,b] [--cutoff ISO] [--ledger L] [--json out]` computes the harness benchmark protocol's per-chain metric: whether the chain completes (`completed` or `completed_scope_declared`, the same in `run_state.json` and `metadata.json`, with a regular `report.md` and no `report_withheld.md`; a missing status is `no_status`, not completed), the intervention types I1-I9 (headline I1+I5+I6+I8; owner decisions I2+I3+I4+I7 beside it; a pause still in force is `pending_pauses`, not an intervention), and owner-wait split into owner response and operator latency, censored at the cut-off. Every count is taken on the chain as it stood at the cut-off (`as_of`: later runs, history, invocations and owner records dropped); the default cut-off is the strict arm's (24 h after the first launch, or the first time every chain is FINAL or paused), and `--cutoff` sets an assisted-arm one. Naive local invocation times are converted to UTC with `metadata.utc_offset_s`; without it, a count or wait that needs one is unavailable. Every field carries `from: record | inferred | unavailable`: pauses without a history entry are inferred from a transition into SIGN, OWNER_DECISION or (after signing) SPEC followed by a resume; relaunch chains come from `metadata.parent_run` (unique name, matching `run_state.json` hash) or the operator's `--chain`; I8 counts a spec changed after sign-off and staged inputs whose hash no longer matches `data/inputs_provenance.json` (inferred until a run-files hash record exists). A run dir that cannot be read is a row with status `unreadable`, never a skipped row; paths under `_truth` or `evals_private` are refused; files are read through `lib.safe_io.read_nofollow`. `load_run`, `as_of`, `chains`, `summarise`, `rows` and `wave_summary` are importable for PR 4B's run harvest and feedback store. Self-tests HM1-37 on synthetic run dirs.
