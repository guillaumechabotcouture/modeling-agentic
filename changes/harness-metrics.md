## Changelog

**Harness metrics: completion, owner interventions and owner-wait per run chain.** New read-only `scripts/harness_metrics.py <run_dir>... [--chain a,b] [--cutoff ISO] [--ledger L] [--json out]` computes the harness benchmark protocol's per-chain metric: whether the chain completes (`completed` or `completed_scope_declared` with `report.md` present and no `report_withheld.md`; a missing status is `no_status`, not completed), the intervention types I1-I9 (headline I1+I5+I6+I8; owner decisions I2+I3+I4+I7 reported beside it), and owner-wait split into owner response and operator latency, censored at the cut-off (default the strict arm's: 24 h after the first launch, or when every chain is FINAL or paused, whichever is first). Naive local invocation times are converted to UTC with `metadata.utc_offset_s`; without it a wait that needs one is not computable. Every field carries `from: record | inferred | unavailable`; pauses without a history entry are inferred from a transition into SIGN or OWNER_DECISION followed by a resume, and relaunch chains come from `metadata.parent_run` or the operator's `--chain`. A run dir that cannot be read is a row with status `unreadable`, never a skipped row; paths under `_truth` or `evals_private` are refused. Files are read through `lib.safe_io.read_nofollow`. `load_run`, `chains`, `summarise`, `rows` and `wave_summary` are importable for PR 4B's run harvest and feedback store. I8 counts a spec changed after sign-off and staged inputs whose hash no longer matches `data/inputs_provenance.json` (inferred until a run-files hash record exists). Self-tests HM1-29 on synthetic run dirs.
