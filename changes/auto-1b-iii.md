## Changelog

**v2 autonomy 1B-iii: under `--signoff lead` an engine departure counts as provisional (owner default D2).** Item 3.b of `docs/design/v2-autonomy-brief.md` PR 1B, as re-audited. A departure from benchmark evidence (`engine_departures`) counted only under the owner's signature, so an unattended lead run whose engine needs one (a held-out benchmark out of band, a component without a benchmark) had no eligible engine and ended `run_failed`. `benchmark_coverage.signature` now returns `provisional_lead` for the lead's signature of the unchanged spec (and for the lead about to sign); `gate` counts departures under it (`COUNTING`) and records each with `status: provisional_lead` (in `engine_choice.json`), still only when its decision test varies the effect. `analysis_backend._departures` and `preconditions._benchmark_eligibility` pass the lead's status through; a spec edited after either signature counts none, as before. `results_lines` heads such departures "Provisional engine departures (signed by the lead; not reviewed by the owner)" and the sign-off sheet says a lead's signature accepts them provisionally. Not loosened: a net-effect posterior exception stays the owner's alone (`preconditions._net_effect_posterior` refuses it under a provisional signature); under `--signoff owner` a lead signature still does not release SIGN. Not in this PR (each needs its own owner yes): effect parameters for the benchmarks without them (3.a) and per-configuration benchmark records (3.c). Self-tests G_LEAD_PROV_DEP, G_LEAD_SIG, G_LEAD_RESULTS (benchmark_coverage), PC14 (preconditions), G5e and G5f rewritten for D2 (analysis_backend).
