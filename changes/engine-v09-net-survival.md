## Changelog

**Malaria engine v0.9: net loss follows the published smooth-compact survival curve, not an exponential.** From v0.3 to v0.8, nets were lost at the constant daily rate ln2 / `net_retention_half`, an exponential with the published median as its half-life. v0.9 uses the curve that the median's source fits (Bertozzi-Villa et al. 2021, Nat Commun, PMC8196080, Supplementary Note 1.4): S(t) = exp(k − k / (1 − (t/L)²)) for t < L, else 0. The paper fixes k at 20, and L = median × sqrt((k + ln2) / ln2), which is 8.96 y at the 1.64 y median. Each day a net of age a is lost with probability 1 − S(a+1)/S(a), using the net age the sim already tracks for holes (`model.net_loss_prob`). `access_for_use` divides by the same S(t). `net_retention_half` keeps its name, value (1.64 y) and card range (1.33–2.37 y) as the median. The new fixed parameter `net_survival_k` = 20 has its reason recorded in `priors.yaml`. Insecticide decay and holes are unchanged. Muleba keeps its trial-derived retention, re-derived for the new shape from the same two reported use values (77.3% in year 1, 59.2% in year 2): 841.3 d instead of 949 d. `priors.yaml` records Nigeria's 2.22 y (2.00–2.47, 9 surveys; Supplementary Table 1.7) under `country_values` for a run spec to cite (`basis: literature`). The engine default stays the 40-country median. No net-survival value was chosen using any benchmark result. Only the existing fitted parameters were refitted, to B10, B11, B12, B12b and B12m. B7, B8 and B16c stay held out. Tests were written first. N0–N5 cover the parameter table, S(0) = 1, S(median) = ½, S = 0 for t ≥ L, the compounded hazard, agent-level loss by net age, access and Muleba's ratio. Codex review round 1 added two more: N6 (a non-positive or non-finite k or median fails closed) and N7 (net age 0 on each campaign step, for first nets and for replacements). EP36 now reads Muleba's own value from the pack. Because the change is not byte-identical, `validate_once.sh 16 12` recomputed all evidence on one Coiled VM (workspace `idm-coiled-idmad-r2`). The joint fit moved to cfp_kill 0.405 and irs_kill 0.433, and the same 14 of 17 benchmarks are in band. Before → after: B7 0.578 → 0.501 and B8 0.449 → 0.410 (both out of band, as before), and B16c stays at 4 of 5 rules. The v0.8 results and fit are kept in `history/*_v0.8.yaml`. Engine self-test V7 (burn-in starting-guess sensitivity, 3% tolerance) failed on the recomputed 6-seed evidence: treated_all −3.2% at m0 3.5, DALYs averted +7.1% at m0 9.0. Codex review round 2 asked for a better-powered run, declared before running it: 24 seeds, with the rule, levels and outputs unchanged. On that run, every output is within 3% (largest move +2.3%, DALYs averted at m0 3.5) and V7 passes. Compute: 78.82 + 19.3 = 98.12 Coiled credits on two one-VM clusters, both stopped.
