## Changelog

**World-model trials: a template check before any seed.** `scripts/world_model_trials.py` gains `template_problems(q)` and `lint_templates(qs)` (CLI `lint-templates`), which read a question's inputs only (the decision spec, its units table, the template's `wtp.yaml` and `engine_reads.yaml`, the willingness-to-pay table and the question text) and run no LLM task, no engine design and no engine run. Five checks: `schema` (toolkit/spec/schema.py load and validate); `independence` (declared-independent features uncorrelated in the realized units table, |Spearman| <= `batch.INDEPENDENCE_RHO_MAX` = 0.3 by `batch.realized_rank_correlations`, plus `batch.independent_features`' generator and basis); `engine_reads` (every declared feature on a bound column or attribute, or declared derived in `engine_reads.yaml`, by `engine_unread_features`, the guard's engine-blind input); `cost_units` (with a willingness to pay, the question's stated US$ budget equals `envelope_usd`, or `wtp.yaml` declares `cost_units: {currency: USD, basis}`; a `prices.currency` other than USD needs its exchange rate: batch 1's flu_vaccine counted costs in doses against a willingness to pay per USD); `shadow_price` (no value in the question, the template or the spec, `wtp.yaml` holds only `outcome_type` and `cost_units`, and the table's sources cite no sweep or harness file). `run_trials` runs it before a question's first seed: a failing template is recorded as `template_refused` with its problems, and no seed task, spend reservation or engine design follows. On the shipped questions file, 57 of 82 questions (45 of 65 templates) fail: engine_reads 42, independence 25, cost_units 10, schema 0, shadow_price 0. Self-tests WT26a-l.
