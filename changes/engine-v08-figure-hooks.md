## Changelog

**Malaria engine v0.8: the figure hooks of `toolkit/engines/hooks.py` (#127).** Two optional hooks for the v2 timeline and trajectory figures, to #127's interface; `contract.py` is unchanged. `schedule(params, unit, package, horizon)` lists the unit's delivery events in one arm over `[horizon.start, window end)`: past net campaigns (`history`), the package's net campaign, SMC rounds, IRS rounds and case-management add-on period (`decision`, the only charged kind), and routine net campaigns and routine SMC rounds (`routine`). It is pure and comes from `delivery_plan()`, the one function that the sim's configuration (`_plan`, `_base_sim`) now also reads, so the schedule cannot drift from what the sim delivers. The card declares `outputs["series"]` (`pfpr_u5`, the calibration metric, plus `pfpr_2_10` and `pfpr_all` as monthly means; `inc_all` and `inc_u5` as episodes per person-year), and `run_series(params, unit, packages, horizon, seed, metrics, step="month")` returns them per package, read from the model's daily records through `run_batch`'s member loop, so packages share the burn-in and history. Window metrics equal the series aggregated over the same window (to 1e-9). `run` and `run_batch` are unchanged: the sims are the same; `benchmarks/restamp_proof.py` compared v0.7 and v0.8 on the pack's inputs before the evidence identity fields were re-stamped. Tests: engine S0–S8, written first. Review round 1 (Codex) and #127's hook contract `figures-hooks/1`: `schedule()` lists deliveries on the daily steps the sim executes (`model.event_steps`, shared with the interventions; same-step targets are one delivery) and fails closed on decision deliveries the sim would not charge (`model.delivery_contains`, shared with `Malaria.in_delivery`), on past campaigns at or after the delivery start and on routine deliveries inside it; `run_series` returns each step's `t_end` and `days` on the contract's grid (`hooks.monthly_grid`; a partial last step when the window ends inside a month), `series_window` reconciles exactly with the window metrics, and each series metric declares a `denominator`; the engine declares `hooks_contract`. The proof was pinned to v0.7 (bf24d1b) and extended with three scenarios the pack does not cover (GC7 delivery with routine nets and SMC, boundary dates and the CM stop, heterogeneous members): 22 of 22 cases identical. Tests: S9-S12, RP10. Review round 2: `series_window` refuses windows outside the series' coverage instead of truncating them (S13); half-open delivery semantics are tested at every boundary against the sim (S14); the proof records each side's Python, NumPy, Starsim and platform and the Coiled cluster and run ids (RP11); and re-stamp records mark the `validate_once.sh` confirmation pending until it runs (RP12). #127 round 3 (exact boundaries): `schedule()` reports and charges each event on the side the sim does, by the sim's own `delivery_contains`, on the real time vector (S15: GC7 decision at d0, past campaign at horizon.start, routine SMC at d1, routine net at campaign + 3, the window end, off-grid starts); a step off a boundary by representation only is reported at the boundary's float without crossing it.
