Allowed: literal PIDs, `%job`, `$!`, `kill -0`, `kill -l`, `kill $(cat <pidfile>)`, and every mention that does not run. On an internal error the command is allowed and the error goes to stderr (a false deny blocks real work). The deny reason names the stop paths: record `echo $! > <run_dir>/models/<name>.pid` at launch and `kill <pid>`; a detached compute batch via `compute.py status <out>` (its `pid`) and `kill <pid>`, which its SIGTERM handler turns into a clean stop (compute.py has no cancel command). One line in the modeler and data-agent prompts. Self-tests KL1-KL3 in main.py (KL3: odd and 1 MB inputs never raise). Retro on the stored traces (Bash summaries, cut at 80 characters; full commands are not stored): attempt 8, 3,202 Bash calls, 5 denied, all `pkill -f` by the modeler (correct); attempt 7, 2,620 calls, 3 denied, all `pkill -f` (correct); no false positives among the other commands that mention kill, pgrep or fuser (mostly `kill -0 $P` polls). Not covered: kills through `sh -c`, variables set from pgrep, `while read`, awk `system()`, PID files written and read in separate calls, and Python `os.kill`. |
