## Gate internals (contributor reference)

This detail used to live in CLAUDE.md. It moved here in Phase 21 ε
because CLAUDE.md is loaded into every pipeline agent's context
(`setting_sources=["project"]`), and agents being graded by the gates
should not be taught the gates' leniency windows.

- **Severity hierarchy**: HIGH blocks STAGE 7 ACCEPT; MEDIUM is a
  warning; LOW is informational. `_check_*` functions in
  `scripts/validate_critique_yaml.py` return
  `[{"kind": str, "severity": "HIGH" | "MEDIUM", "claim": str, ...}]`.
- **Round-aware consolidation** (Phase 10 ψ): during the drafting
  window one `allocation_rigor_in_progress` MEDIUM replaces separate
  `*_missing` findings; round-aware MEDIUM→HIGH escalation is Phase 12 β.
- **Drafting window** (Phase 19 α/γ/δ): round 1 demotes effort-floor,
  benchmark, and sufficiency findings to MEDIUM; round ≥ 2 honors
  manifest severities.
- **Scope declarations**: `scope_declarable: false` in
  `.claude/orchestration/rigor_artifacts.yaml` /
  `.claude/orchestration/effort_floors.yaml` marks findings that a
  `scope_declaration.yaml` entry cannot silence (Phase 15 α, Phase 18 α,
  Phase 19 α). Budget cap: default 3, hard ceiling 5 (Phase 19 β).
